**Proposed wall/cleanup:** absolute parent wall deadline3600s, gate allowance600s. Each launch receives min(phase allowance, parent remaining minus cleanup reserve). Cleanup runs in finally, remains inside the shared wall/CPU limits and preserves the first error. **Actual CPU without a live-child estimate:** canonical final ledger is ΔparentSELF.user+system + ΔreapedCHILDREN.user+system in a fresh dedicated parent. The worker checks its own measured process CPU from entry through imports/stages/iterations/finalization against its issued allowance. While it is live, parent separately guards its own measured SELF delta since launch against the reserve, terminating early if cleanup margin is threatened. Child heartbeats are diagnostic, not a claim to know current global usage; no wall×threads estimate or missing-child-as-zero accounting. After reap recompute the actual cumulative ledger before another launch. Before production acceptance add independently measured verifier invocation CPU once; production≤3300 and verifier/finalization≤remaining300, total≤3600. Cooperative checks may discover overrun late; record actual overrun/ineligible failure, never claim a hard mathematical cap. Reserves are reallocated from actual remaining after reap, not repeatedly subtracted or counted as spent.
