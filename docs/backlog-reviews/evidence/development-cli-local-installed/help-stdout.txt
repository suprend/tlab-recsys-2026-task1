                            --validation-acceptance VALIDATION_ACCEPTANCE
                            --output OUTPUT --max-iterations MAX_ITERATIONS
                            --train-query-cap TRAIN_QUERY_CAP
                            --max-matrix-rows MAX_MATRIX_ROWS --depth DEPTH

                            CPU_SECONDS --wall-seconds WALL_SECONDS
                            --rss-bytes RSS_BYTES --learning-rate
                            LEARNING_RATE --l2-leaf-reg L2_LEAF_REG

Fit one new development model and select its prefix on separate validation.
Limits are cooperative process observations, not hard whole-process-tree
limits. External supervision must include interpreter startup, blocking calls
and cleanup. Historical frozen/smoke trainers have separate contracts and are
unchanged.

options:
