TASK
Sessions of an online shop. An event is (session, item, ts, event) with event in {view, cart, order}; ts is in
seconds. Each session of the target week is cut at a point that does not depend on its events: the prefix is
known, the rest is hidden. For every session and every event type t the model ranks candidate items, and the
first 20 count. Labels: view - the first viewed item after the cut; cart - items put into the cart after the
cut; order - items ordered after the cut. R_t = sum over sessions of |top 20 with labels of type t| divided by
the sum over sessions of min(20, |labels of type t|). Score = 0.10*R_view + 0.30*R_cart +
0.60*R_order. The feedback below carries a coarse recall on a validation sample (described there).

- Union: (h), then p_view, p_cart, p_order without repeats, at most 110 per session.
