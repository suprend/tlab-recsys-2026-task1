Implementation candidate for one historically identified failed observer invocation. This does not rerun the producer, create a fit allowance, change the four models or twelve checkpoint looks, alter model/metric/selection rules, or open reserved outcomes. Original failed receipts and logs stay immutable and remain failed.

The initial controller stopped because the observer's mechanical plan rederivation invoked CPython's macOS environment snapshot. Its platform query launched `uname -p` and `file -b <python>`, contradicting the observer's absolute no-CHILDREN rule. Two fresh environment-only probes reproduced the side effect and the frozen guard's ValueError, without data/models. A separately reviewed DarwinARM64 adapter derives and checks the exact platform in process and primes only the stdlib platform cache. The no-subprocess rule and original numerical/data/model audit functions remain unchanged in the additive recovery observer. Other platforms are unsupported by this recovery, not silently adapted.

`run_strong_recovery.py` requires an external exact source/policy approval, creates one exclusive global zero-fit observer claim before launch and supervises one isolated process group using the accepted lifecycle helper. It cannot call a producer or fit. Every original failure/producer byte seal and both old final/failed receipts stay pinned. The old controller charged its small failed-marker persistence before writing its final report; only its three finite nondecreasing SELF/total/wall counters may differ. All other fields and complete key sets must match canonically.

An observer failure consumes this recovery; there is no third replay, replacement fit, fresh source repair or automatic model promotion. Known original/recovery evidence changes, unknown usage, deadline or RSS violations fail closed. Successful output remains `recovery_verified_pending_external_acceptance` until a reviewer checks the reaped observer, external exit-tail ledger, complete original/recovery resource accounting and score/metric/winner evidence.
