## FB-05 — wall-clock high-frequency activity energy

**Paper mechanism.** WEARec's November2025 preprint/AAAI2026 paper combines dynamic frequency filtering and wavelet reconstruction of sequential item embeddings. It does not propose our activity-count scalar. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.07028v2), [author repository](https://github.com/xhy963319431/WEARec). An independent December2025 BSARec reproduction finds no clear advantage of DSP over a simple residual connection and material padding effects, providing a useful counterexample to automatic frequency-method enthusiasm. [Primary reproduction](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.17442v1).

**Adaptation.** For a candidate's numeric category c and user u, partition `[t−7d,t)` into32 equal bins of18,900seconds. Let h_j be past view rows at historical `(u,c)` in bin j, with the candidate category read from its latest global metadata bucket before t. Compute two Haar-like detail energies: `E1=Σ_j(h_2j−h_(2j+1))²/N²`; `E2=Σ_j(h_4j+h_(4j+1)−h_(4j+2)−h_(4j+3))²/N²`, N=Σh. These measure temporal roughness at two fixed scales. They are not an event-index Fourier transform and do not establish daily or weekly periodicity.

Emit two numeric cores and a missing flag if needed; N and complete-source coverage are reused only when their exact existing semantics match. At N=0 or incomplete7d source coverage, energies are missing rather than fabricated from zero padding. A short-observed user inside a fully covered source interval is a distinct support state retained from B. No bin edges, frequencies or filters are fitted.

**Novelty / counter.** Current counts, one trend/decay and FB-01 action order do not determine within-window roughness. Embed four temporal bins after28 common zero bins: h=(3,0,2,1) versus h=(2,1,2,1). Both total6, occupy only the final21hours and can share the first/last event times. The unnormalized E1 numerators are10 versus2. With one fixed user/item/category, unchanged other actions and no duplicates, 24h/7d totals, distinct support and final recencies match. Counterhypothesis: energy is merely sparsity, bin-phase artifact or repeated logging, and trees plus ordinary subwindow counts perform as well.

**Cheapest falsification and control.** Literal histograms first, including a one-second boundary, global peers, N=0 and insufficient coverage. Shift all anchors/events together and require invariance; changing bin phase alone is an explicitly different representation. A future equal-column comparison uses incumbent+energies versus incumbent+two fixed fine-window count contrasts on identical Q/C/Y and learner settings. Also report whether gain persists after a prespecified same-count event-jitter diagnostic; the diagnostic is not a new quality selection loop. The reproduction paper's padding result warns against claiming a spectral mechanism when coverage alone changed.

**Finite cost / availability.** Up to three added columns per arm. A 100-query source feasibility proposal caps matched history1Mrows,100,000 active user-category keys,30 CPU minutes and4GB peak RSS, with exact32-bin state and query materialization charged; overflow stops without incomplete output. At most two later quality fits/90 CPU minutes, separately reserved. Exact anchor-relative bins generally need requested-history replay or a suitable exact bucket structure; a coarse calendar snapshot is not silently equivalent. All source events are `<t`; same-second buckets update after queries. Quality, useful support and throughput are unmeasured.

## FB-06 — robust consensus of session interest profiles

**Paper mechanism.** GROS,23 February2024, aggregates group estimators in a metric space by choosing a center with a small strict-majority neighborhood radius; §3 permits minimization over the observed group estimators. Its statistical guarantees rely on assumptions including independent group estimators. [Primary paper, §§2–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.15442v1). July2025 HyPro reports that removing putative noise can hurt short-session recommendation and proposes both removal and enhancement channels. [Primary article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41019-025-00301-1). Our features do not implement either paper's full learner.

**Adaptation.** Select the five most recently observed nonempty view-session prefixes of u touching the past7d window. Each q_s is a distribution over historical category IDs of that session's in-window views. Missing session IDs cannot be invented; unknown category is an explicit mass bucket. Current session tails are excluded at t, and none of these prefixes is asserted completed. Select q* among these five profiles minimizing distance to its third-nearest profile, using total-variation distance and a fixed recency tie rule. Candidate affinity is q* at the candidate's as-of category. Compare it to the equal-session arithmetic mean affinity from exactly the same five prefixes.

The first proposed model bundle has affinity, number of eligible observed sessions capped at5, and missing flag; no bonus dispersion column is hidden. At fewer than5 eligible sessions affinity is missing in both arms. Source history is retained unchanged. GROS guarantees do not transfer automatically: shopping sessions are dependent, selected by recency and nonstationary; session identity is not a reliable person/device boundary.

**Novelty / counter.** This selects a consensus *profile* before projecting the candidate; it is not row clipping, duplicate collapsing, audience concentration FB-02, or a shrinkage prior. Existing event/unique-product weighting and session-presence features do not implement this aggregation. Literal five binary-category profiles `[1,1,1,0,0]` versus `[.6,.6,.6,.6,.6]` have the same equal-session mean.6 and can have identical pooled category counts with five events per session, but majority-neighborhood centers1 versus.6. This distinguishes the robust center from the matched equal-session mean. Counterhypothesis: the minority sessions contain emerging demand, so consensus suppresses the useful signal or is mostly unavailable.

**Cheapest falsifier / matched pilot.** Check those profiles, deterministic ties, an unknown-category bucket, missing session IDs and a single newly observed session. First compare equal-session mean and consensus with exactly the same eligibility/mask/support, not consensus against event-weighted mean. The latter would conflate changed weighting with robustness. Prespecify short-history and shifting-category slices; no positive robustness claim follows merely from synthetic outlier resistance. A future pilot is100Q,≤1M matched rows,≤100,000 retained session-category entries,30 CPU minutes/4GB; five-profile25-distance matrices are small but source retrieval may dominate. Two matched fits/90 CPU minutes are separately proposed. No fitted encoder or threshold; profile availability is event-prefix availability at t. Measured gain/support: none.

## FB-07 — uncertainty-aware acquisition over typed programs

**Paper mechanism.** The January2026 Human–LLM Collaborative Feature Engineering paper, published at ICLR2026, separates proposal generation from a Bayesian utility/uncertainty surrogate and UCB selection; it can selectively request pairwise human preferences. Original operation encodings include an OpenAI embedding API, and utility requires learner refits. [Primary paper, §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.21060v1). Neither the embedding calls, human feedback nor iterative model-evaluation budget is assumed available here. The paper's confidence lemma assumes error behavior; its existence does not validate a small custom surrogate's intervals.

**Adaptation / novelty.** In a fixed pool of at most32 typed legal ASTs, encode ordered primitive/path tokens in at most64 declared dimensions. Maintain an exact Bayesian linear regression posterior over a *frozen fit-free development information proxy*, then choose two additional program evaluations by UCB after six shared initial probes. Keep actual AST identity for caches/dedup; vector collisions never mean semantic equality. No operation is accepted merely because its posterior mean rises. Final selected≤3-column closures are tested by separately reserved ranker fits.

FA01 scores feature pairs; FA02 learns family transfer edges; FA03 shares programs across tasks; FA04 induces proposal macros. FB-07 instead learns uncertainty across program descriptors to allocate the next expensive proxy evaluation. It differs from HYP013's residual correction model and HYP014's unavailable external pretrained selector. This is a local acquisition heuristic, not a claim of calibrated Bayesian quality prediction. Conditioning information on a few B summaries remains weaker than utility conditional on the full incumbent.

**Counter / literal falsifier.** With only six probes and64 dimensions, priors may dominate and high uncertainty may direct the selector toward useless programs. Use a two-dimensional literal surrogate: six observations only constrain coordinate1. Two unobserved programs have equal mean but different posterior variance; UCB should choose the uncertain one while a fixed greedy tie rule chooses the other. Give the uncertain program true proxy gain+1 in one synthetic world and−1 in another. This verifies exploration and explicitly defeats a universal-benefit claim. Add two distinct ASTs with equal encodings to ensure the cache still evaluates both identities. This is not a fit on real rows.

**Matched cost and temporal fit.** UCB versus seeded random gets the same six initial observed scores, then two paid evaluations each, from the same fixed pool. Charge all baseline proxy estimation, posterior updates, materialization, unsuccessful proposals and failed evaluations:16 opportunity slots across policies, with honest shared-cache information. Proposed mechanics gate≤32 literal ASTs/0 downstream fits/5 CPU minutes/256MiB. Later source+proxy work≤30 CPU minutes/4GB and two final equal-closure model fits≤90 CPU minutes require new authorization. If feedback is actual NDCG rather than the frozen proxy, six initial refits cannot be declared free and this design is deferred under a two-fit ceiling. Bins/proxy labels mature before selector fit; selector compute completes before later application; current R2 never gets reselected. Existing inspected development can support screening only.
