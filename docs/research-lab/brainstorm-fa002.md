## Source acceptance and rejected starting points

[Set-Encoder, arXiv v5/ECIR2025, §§3.1–3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.06912v5) implements permutation-invariant inter-passage attention. The [official repository](https://github.com/webis-de/set-encoder) identifies its author implementation in [lightning-ir](https://github.com/webis-de/lightning-ir/blob/main/lightning_ir/models/cross_encoders/set_encoder.py): candidate interaction states become cross-candidate keys/values, with a self-exclusion mask. Its own experiments are counterevidence to automatic optimism: ordinary relevance tasks did not necessarily benefit from interactions; novelty-specific training made them useful. We borrow candidate-set dependence and permutation symmetry, not text inputs, attention weights or empirical gains. No missing candidates are synthesized; the author code’s stochastic underfull-set padding is not copied.

[CycleNet, NeurIPS2024, §3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.18479v1) explicitly aligns recurring patterns by cycle position, then forecasts residuals. Its [official author code](https://github.com/ACAT-SCUT/CycleNet/blob/main/models/CycleNet.py) uses learned recurrent-cycle parameters and modulo indexing. Our deterministic complex moments below learn neither its cycle table nor its forecasting model. Their relevance to purchase ranking is unmeasured.

LiGR was an initial search lead, but the [current author record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03417) records withdrawal on8February2026 after discrepancies in claims were found. It is rejected as empirical support. DeepHalo author-code contents could not be verified and are not required here. Competitor-choice exposure-correction methods require displayed alternatives; REES46 has no impressions, so no such interpretation is imported.

## FA05 — known-subset rival commitment inside a fixed candidate set

**Mechanism.** For query q=(u,t), deduplicated fixed C and current past-resolved category c_t(i), define G_i={j∈C\{i}:c_t(j) is resolved and c_t(j)=c_t(i)}. Let x_j be the existing raw pair_cart_7d(u,j,t). Emit:

- F_i=log(1+max_{j∈G_i}x_j), with empty resolved matching subset→0;
- n_i=|G_i|;
- z_q=number of unresolved-category candidates / |C|, including the focal candidate.

If the focal category is unresolved, F_i/n_i are NULL. Their missingness must exactly alias the existing B candidate_category_missing flag. Every x_j must be numeric, nonnegative and finite under the existing known-empty count contract; a missing parent row/count is an integrity failure, never silently ignored by max. If exact mask reuse cannot be proved, defer this three-column closure. z is a query constant that can interact with candidate-specific F/n, not a standalone ranker. Empty C emits no candidate rows and retains Q.

**New information and scope.** This adds a typed QuerySetReduce primitive over C, rather than another pointwise arithmetic AST. The historical grammar has entity aggregates, distincts, recencies and ratios, but cannot read another candidate’s pair feature while scoring i. HYP001/067 graph compatibility and HYP053 category quotas concern other relations or candidate construction. F001’s directed category transition graph is also different. Category equality is only a proxy for rivalry; C is a retrieved set, not demonstrated co-exposure or a validated set of substitutes. A zero describes the **known subset**, even if unresolved rivals exist.

**Literal nonalias.** Freeze C={i,j,l}, categories A,A,B; outside products k,m have A,B. All users/products already exist with identical metadata and views. At one strict-past timestamp τ, history H1 adds carts u→j,m and v→k,l; H2 adds u→k,l and v→j,m. Every focal(i,u) B41+R2 field remains equal. User cart totals/category-cart totals, each item’s cart total, and competitor pair-cart multiset{1,0} are equal. Candidate-wide maximum is1 in both. Yet F_i is log2 versus0. This is an operator-level fixed-C witness, not proof that re-running retrieval would emit the same C: a full-pipeline fixture must independently preserve the original candidate manifest.

**Counter and cheapest falsifiers.** F may merely proxy category-level commitment, or most queries may have zero competitor carts. Test the printed witness; candidate-order permutation; tied maxima with self-exclusion; a single category member; unknown rival versus unknown focal category; and a nonnumeric parent fault. Top-two maxima must preserve ties and product identity. Permuting within one category cannot falsify this max; a useful placebo moves competitor cart states across category groups while preserving their multiset. These are planned tiny literals, not executed tests.

**Matched quality question.** Primary is frozen current W=B41+R2. Compare W+F/n/z with W+user-category-cart7d/n/z on identical Q/C/fullY, masks, population, weights and CatBoost settings/iteration. The control’s category-cart count uses historical event-attached category, without current-category backfill; its extra source work is paid. The literal witness preserves that control too. A gain only over the pooled control, without a gain over W, does not promote the feature. Cross-category placebo scoring is a prespecified exploratory diagnostic; an extra trained placebo would consume a fit and cannot be added freely.

**Finite work.** First future source gate:100 label-independent Q,≤22,000 parent rows,≤22,000 category/top-two states,≤1M retained user-event/category keys. With authenticated complete parent F and category context, the set reduction adds0 raw-source scans; reading/authentication is charged. The pooled control or missing metadata requires one declared projection pass over [source_start,max t), at most43M examined raw rows, with actions counted only in [t−168h,t). These are scan ceilings, not throughput predictions. Hashing, scans, sorting, oracle and failures share≤30actualCPUmin/4GiB before any fit. Admission also needs≥20 of100Q with at least two distinct F values among known focal rows; failing this conservative screen means defer, not scientific rejection.

## FA06 — daily phase compatibility of user views and item purchases

**Mechanism.** With ω=2π/86400 and s measured in canonical UTC seconds modulo86400, compute raw event-weighted means over [t−168h,t):

U_u=Σ_user views exp(iωs)/N_u; I_i=Σ_item purchases exp(iωs)/N_i.

Emit alignment a_ui=Re(U_u conjugate(I_i)), coherence r_ui=|U_u||I_i| and one shared missing flag m=1[N_u=0 or N_i=0]. Both scalars are NULL when m=1. Positive support with zero complex resultant gives genuine a=r=0. Bounds are −r≤a≤r≤1. Denominators are exactly user_view7d and R2 item_purchase7d already in W; no new denominator is hidden. The3-column closure must share one identical missing mask instead of uncharged per-core flags.

**Novelty.** Counts discard clock phase; recency retains only the latest event. This new sinusoidal weighted aggregate cannot be generated by the historical count/distinct/recency/ratio grammar. It is not FA01’s synergy selector, FA02 migration, FA03 task transfer, FA04 library induction or any FB primitive. It is also not F001’s repeat waiting-time survival curve: no repeat spell, hazard, censoring estimator or fitted state is formed. Adding sine/cosine mean summaries plus their products would algebraically subsume the alignment; the nonalias claim is specifically against the verified historical baseline.

**Literal nonalias with equal coherence.** Set t=day8 at00UTC. User u views only off-candidate product a at day2h00, day3h00, day7h18 in H1; change only the middle event to day3h18 in H2. Item i has three fixed purchases by another user at day2h00, day3h00, day7h18. Keep first observations, latest metadata and category fixed. The older event shift crosses no24h/7d boundary. All focal B41+R2 counts, supports, first-seen ages and latest recencies agree. U_1=I=(2−i)/3, U_2=(1−2i)/3. Coherence is5/9 in both; alignment changes5/9→4/9. Thus the new phase information is not just concentration. This six-event-per-world construction is an analytic witness, not a model result.

**Counter and falsifier.** Daily24h targets cover a whole cycle, so phase matching may be irrelevant. Sparse phases may proxy geography, item activity or logging habits; unknown individual local time is not inferred. A common clock-origin rotation cancels algebraically, but per-user timezone heterogeneity does not. Test the equal-coherence witness, common-origin rotation invariance, equal positive-support phases cancelling to zero, exact lower/upper boundary and global-peer/future-suffix invariance. Fix period24h once; searching12/24/48/168h or offsets would be a new paid discovery branch.

**Matched quality question.** Use two fits: W+coherence/missing control versus W+alignment/coherence/missing treatment, and report both against frozen W. This deliberately adds just the phase interaction to a common nuisance basis; matching an arbitrary column count with noise would obscure the question. Same train Q/C/Y/weights/settings/checkpoint and full-Y evaluator throughout. A separately declared phase-scramble diagnostic may rotate item phases by a fixed outcome-blind hash, preserving amplitudes/support; no seed retry or extra fit is free.

**Finite work.**100Q/≤22,000 pairs; read user views and all-user purchases of requested items, including off-C user views. One projection scan [min t−168h,max t), bounded by43M raw rows actually examined, even if storage pruning fails. State≤1M sparse(entity,UTC-hour-bin) keys,≤22,100 entity summaries; each hour stores count and sine/cosine sums. Midnight anchors permit exact completed-hour aggregation; arbitrary-anchor support would need a new exact boundary contract. No dense user×item array. Source/oracle/loading/failures≤30actualCPUmin/4GiB, then STOP. Require≥20/100Q with at least two defined alignment values differing within query; report purchases/views support and full168h coverage. This gate can reject a useful rare signal and proves no power.
