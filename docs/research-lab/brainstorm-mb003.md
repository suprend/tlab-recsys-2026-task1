## MB3-01 — learned edge functions → audited symbolic internal features

**Source facts.** [KAN2.0](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.10205v1) learns one-dimensional edge functions, introduces multiplication nodes, and presents module and symbolic extraction for scientific problems. Its physics demonstrations do not establish a purchase-ranking advantage. The authors' [pykan code](https://github.com/KindXiaoming/pykan) supports CPU examples; this is not evidence about our population or runtime.

In [MultKAN.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KindXiaoming/pykan/master/kan/MultKAN.py), `suggest_symbolic` fits each allowed atom to an edge and balances fidelity against complexity; `auto_symbolic` traverses edges, with default R² threshold zero. `symbolic_formula` reconstructs expressions with optional normalizers. Default training loss is MSE; Adam and LBFGS are available. The activation cache and checkpoint can contain input data; `speed()` disables symbolic and activation collection. These defaults require an explicit bounded adapter, not a turnkey recommendation pipeline.

[utils.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KindXiaoming/pykan/master/kan/utils.py) fits `c*f(a*x+b)+d`: an a/b grid, covariance-based R² proxy, then linear regression for c/d. Defaults are 101×101 and three zooms, constructing an N×grid×grid tensor. Symbolization therefore contains parameter fits and expensive scoring of proposals.

**Own adaptation and novelty boundary.** Fit one small generator on an earlier, label-mature warmup. Export at most two *internal modules*, not its final prediction. Numerically learned spline edges are locally fitted to identity/square/tanh atoms; retain a formula only after typing, algebraic canonicalization, and domain/missingness checks. The mechanism under test is learning a continuous scaffold before discrete symbolic extraction. It is not a claim that squares/products, neural features, or model-guided search are new. HYP002 typed grammar, IIFE synergy, TopoFE islands, LILO compression and the explicitly excluded MPL family remain the comparators; an expression already in their grammar is an alias unless the proposer earns a measured budget advantage. Exporting the final score would collapse to M08's upstream-score feature.

Predeclare eight registry parents and their units/missingness/scaling policy without label-based parent search. Use at most four hidden nodes, including one binary multiplication node, spline grid5/order3, one seed and ≤1024 Adam minibatch updates of ≤512 rows. The two exported modules' combined backward cone may contain ≤12 active edges; expanded formulas have ≤12 primitive nodes each. No fallback library, width, seed or threshold search follows failure. Fixed query-macro BCE is an **unimplemented adaptation**: the source fit defaults do not supply indexed query weights. A bounded custom training adapter must preserve query sampling/weights rather than silently use row BCE.

**Own literal falsifiers.** First, `((x+y)^2-x^2-y^2)/2 = x*y`: exact rational checks on all 25 integer pairs in [−2,2] agree. An elaborate discovered expression may be a grammar duplicate. That finite check supplements the algebraic identity; it does not prove equivalence with NULLs, overflow or arbitrary float evaluation. Canonicalization includes parent identities, normalizers and missingness semantics, not just matching values on a small sample.

Second, excellent average fidelity can still reverse a boundary. Let a numerical module be h(x,z)=x+2e−6*z and its approximation g(x,z)=x+4e−7. At x=[−1,−.5,0,.5,1], z=[0,0,1,0,0], ordinary R² is 0.99999999999872. The literal candidate pair A=(0,1), B=(1e−6,0) has numerical values (2e−6,1e−6) and symbolic values (4e−7,1.4e−6), reversing its order. The errors are tiny; the margin is smaller. These are constructed functions, not a trained KAN result or the code's regularized R² score. If this module drives a score, placing the pair at positions20/21 makes the risk relevant to top20. Thus neither local R² nor AST size is an admission statistic. Audit absolute errors and margin crossings on a separate chronological warmup audit slice, including mature positive-support queries, before freezing extraction.

**Maturity and counterhypothesis.** All generator labels, scaler fits, pruning choices, affine atom fits and audit decisions must be mature and finished at availability `a0`. Downstream B41 rows start strictly after a0 in every arm; no warmup-trained state is backfilled over earlier training queries. Use one predeclared earliest eligible warmup of ≤2000 queries/≤440000 candidate rows; require ≥32 positive queries and ≥16 positive users before any generator fit. These are proposed support gates, with feasibility unknown. Do not search prefixes until one passes. A shortened common downstream training population changes the comparison, so refresh B41 on exactly that population. Frozen current heldouts cannot supply a new decision.

The counterhypothesis is that a strong tree model already represents these interactions; any gain comes from another upstream predictor or more search attempts. The future package has five learner attempts: generator; fresh B41; B41+raw frozen modules; B41+symbolic modules; B41+random-grammar modules. The grammar arm shares parents, atoms, expanded-node/feature caps and warmup proxy; ≤32 ASTs receive ≤32 coefficient updates each and a one-CPU-hour proposer ceiling. All candidate scores and coefficient fits count. This matches finite search resources, not identical optimization geometry. Raw modules distinguish useful learned representation from successful symbolic compression. If only raw modules help, close the symbolic claim and route that evidence to representation work.

**Finite prospective cost.** A bounded symbolizer calls the lower-level fitter with cache64, grid21, two zooms, ≤12 edges and three atoms: ≤36 affine fits and 2032128 function/grid cells, reusing chosen parameters. One float32 grid buffer is ≈0.108MiB, excluding temporaries. Stock defaults over 5.317m rows would require ≈202GiB for just one such buffer; never feed the full matrix to this fitter. The bounded adapter itself is not yet implemented.

Two full train columns are ≈40.6MiB. Evaluating two 12-node formulas over the supplied train+selection ceilings entails ≤147219576 primitive evaluations, before grammar scoring, raw-network scoring, feature preparation, sorting, uncertainty or B41 fits. Proposed total stop ceilings are 6 CPU-hours, 4 wall-hours, 8GiB peak and two threads: generator1h; grammar coefficient fitting/scoring1h; symbolization/audit0.25h; four downstream fits0.75h each; remaining preparation/scoring0.75h. These allocations sum to6h and are caps, not runtime predictions. Any cap failure terminates the package without expansion. In a separately authorized fresh development reservation, require positive paired NDCG improvement over both fresh B41 and grammar, with a predeclared uncertainty/Recall rule; raw-module comparison must retain acceptable fidelity. No superiority, feasibility or throughput is claimed now.

**Verdict:** conditional proposer, not admitted pilot. First paid step would require a concrete temporal/support contract and the bounded adapter; zero-data alias/fidelity fixtures come first.

## MB3-02 — LA-NAM posterior coupling is not yet a valid interaction selector

**Source facts.** [Improving Neural Additive Models with Bayesian Principles](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/bouchiat24a.html) is an ICML2024 paper (initial preprint2023). Its [Eq17](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.16905v3#S3.SS2.SSS3) uses Gaussian posterior parameter mutual information, `−½ log(1−Corr²)`, to propose pair modules. This is posterior dependence of learned parameters, distinct from IIFE's feature-label synergy. It is neither a direct test of functional nonadditivity nor exposure-corrected recommendation evidence.

The inspected author [interaction.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fortuinlab/LA-NAM/main/src/laplace_skorch/callbacks/interaction.py) contradicts that equation: `_mutual_information` uses unsquared correlation. `_marginal_likelihood_slack` does square normalized precision entries. Stage-two selection zero-fills the diagonal/lower triangle with `triu(...,diagonal=1)` and appends the returned pairs without eligibility filtering. Its last-layer curvature constructs scalar output-multiplier `FullLaplace` on detached unary outputs; it does not pass the main model's learned prior precisions. Pair modules trigger another training stage. [topk_unravel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fortuinlab/LA-NAM/main/src/laplace_skorch/utils.py) flattens the whole matrix, including those excluded zeros. Author code was checked on mutable main; a later implementation must pin and recheck it.

**Own literal blocking witnesses.** Both Σ=[[1,.8],[.8,1]] and its sign-flipped off-diagonal version are positive definite, eigenvalues .2/1.8. Paper MI equals .510825624 for either. Current code gives .804718956 for +.8 and −.293893332 for −.8. For D=2,k=1 the latter masked matrix is [[0,−.293893332],[0,0]]: top1 must select an excluded coordinate. Its tie choice is backend-dependent, but its ineligibility is certain. Squaring correlation fixes sign invariance, yet at Σ=I all valid and excluded zeros tie; eligibility remains unproved. Require explicit i<j indexing or −infinity masking, distinct pairs and a deterministic tie rule.

A separate counterexample survives those repairs. Let X1=X2=t and true logit=t. The target is unary. Precision P=[[5,4],[4,5]] gives Σ=(1/9)[[5,−4],[−4,5]], hence correlation −.8, correct MI .510825624 and precision slack1.021651248. Redundant unary multipliers compensate, producing strong posterior coupling without an identifiable distinct-feature interaction. A two-input network can also fit this unary effect. Strong dependence therefore does not establish a new useful pair. Before predictive selection, reject exact/affine aliases, inspect joint support, and require a supported crossed-input nonadditivity contrast. This is particularly material for nested historical counts and shared-zero parents.

**Disposition and conditional lifecycle cost.** Reject admission of the inspected selector; no source repair is proposed in this task. Cheapest next gate is matrix-only: ±correlation, identity and nearly singular positive-definite cases; check symmetry/PSD, factorization residual/jitter bounds, sign invariance, finite nonnegative scores and exactly k eligible distinct pairs. A corrected MI policy and a marginal-likelihood policy are separate predeclared choices, not free attempts after an outcome.

If all gates later pass, a finite *new* proposal would cap parents at8, warmup at2000 whole queries/440000 rows, and selected pairs at2. Shared unary fit, selected-pair stage, random-pair stage, then fresh B41/B41+selected shapes/B41+random shapes are six learner attempts. The two pair stages start from the same unary state; count ≤128 minibatch updates per stage, ≤2 streamed curvature passes total, and every empirical-Bayes update (≤1 per stage). Reuse MB3-01's maturity/support gates and fresh chronological decision population. A hypothetical 6 CPU-hour/4 wall-hour/8GiB/two-thread ceiling must include all six fits and full feature/score preparation; feasibility is unknown and no budget is allocated now.

Even scalar-output last-layer scoring costs O(ND²), O(D²) covariance storage and O(D³) inversion. At D41,N5.317m this is 8937877000 outer-product cells to score820 pairs, not820 cheap independent numbers. The reduced D8,N440000 ceiling is28160000 cells, plus unary-network evaluation and training curvature. Main-network Laplace fitting can cost more. The source's GPU experiments and default lifecycle do not certify CPU feasibility. This finding creates no new model family: only a repaired selector could earn distinct value over random pairs and the existing learned-selection/grammar controls through future fullY NDCG20, Recall20 and user-cluster evidence.

**Verdict:** negative admission finding. The useful result is a cheap, literal rejection gate and a coupling-versus-interaction distinction, not another candidate name.
