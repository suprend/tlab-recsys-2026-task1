## Common temporal, comparison and cost contract

Raw events retain bags and unordered timestamp peers; features/history use only events strictly before each anchor. Coverage, unavailable state, observed zero and missing support stay distinct. Learned state, search feedback, preprocessor, model, bank/index and source-origin provenance receive explicit fit/maturity/build/available_at clocks. A standalone learner must exist before evaluation; a claimed time-aligned historical state must already exist at that historical anchor. No retrospectively available state is called online-available.

Feature/learner interventions hold Q/C/fullY fixed within their matched contrast. Retrieval interventions intentionally change C: hold Q/fullY, catalog/support and total admission policy fixed, report native actual sizes/overlap/gained and lost targets, then a label-blind matched-size diagnostic. Do not insert positives, refill after failure or shrink primaryY to the retriever's support. Empty mature queries and outside-C/cold purchases remain in the applicable estimands. Candidate Recall is primary for a retrieval stage; full-Y conditional query NDCG20 is primary for a ranker contrast, with Recall20 guard−.002 and the four prior estimands/user-cluster uncertainty. New metrics/support minima are frozen before outcomes.

Baseline means the eligible strong same-domain B/W incumbent. B41 is a useful fixed-input mechanism control; current W44 is not silently ignored when claiming an improvement over our best procedure. New B/W retraining, teacher/encoder/curve estimation, source scans, failed attempts, matrices, all validation looks and output verification are paid. A source row/state/output cap does not bound physical discovery scans. Current study budgets are not reusable leftovers. The linked round ceilings are **future alternatives**, not six additive grants; exact inclusive CPU/wall/RSS/disk and per-arm preparation reserves require a separate plan.
