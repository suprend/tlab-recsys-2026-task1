## MB3-01 — refine; conditional proposer, no pilot admission

[KAN2.0](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.10205v1) supports learned edge functions, multiplication and extraction of modules/symbolic formulas. This is a plausible source for continuous-scaffold search. Its scientific demonstrations establish neither recommendation gain nor this adapter's feasibility. A proper internal module is distinguishable from M08's exported final predictor. Local symbolization still distills a supervised representation; the distinction is where extraction occurs. It does not make its resulting arithmetic a new feature family. Algebraic, affine, parent/missingness and normalization aliases must be rejected against the typed grammar. LILO's archive-induced macros and this label-trained scaffold are different proposal mechanisms, but a measured search-budget advantage is still needed when they produce the same formula.

Three source details need correction or executable closure. [MultKAN.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KindXiaoming/pykan/master/kan/MultKAN.py), `suggest_symbolic`/`auto_symbolic`, fits every candidate atom and then refits the winner. Its stock training enables grid updates and evaluates test labels during each step. `speed()` disables symbolic evaluation and detailed edge collection; forward still retains input/node caches. Symbolization reads the current activation cache. Therefore explicitly refresh the permitted cache, keep numeric targets separate from the symbolic copy, and clear cached inputs before portable serialization. The shipped helper is not the proposed bounded, query-weighted engine.

[KANLayer.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KindXiaoming/pykan/master/kan/KANLayer.py), `update_grid_from_samples`, calls `curve2coef`: moving grids introduces coefficient refits beyond Adam steps. Disable grid adaptation after declared initialization, or charge a finite number of such solves and their rows/edges. Neither a minibatch-update ceiling nor a 12-edge export cone accounts for them.

The source [fitter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KindXiaoming/pykan/master/kan/utils.py) searches affine input parameters and then performs output linear regression. Its covariance proxy has denominator `+1e-4`, so it is scale sensitive and differs from ordinary R². An additional literal exposes this: for x=(-1,0,1), y=10⁻⁶x, the identity with c=10⁻⁶,d=0 fits exactly, ordinary R²=1, yet the proxy at a=1,b=0 is approximately 3.99999984×10⁻⁸. Freeze normalization and the precise score/threshold; reject constant and near-constant targets. A default zero threshold cannot certify fidelity.

The author's alias and boundary witnesses check out. The product identity holds on all 25 exact rational integer pairs and algebraically over reals; NULL/overflow/float evaluation remains a separate contract. For h=x+2×10⁻⁶z and g=x+4×10⁻⁷, the stated cache yields SSE=3.2×10⁻¹², SST=2.5+3.2×10⁻¹² and R²=0.99999999999872, yet A/B ordering reverses. This proves local average fidelity does not preserve order. Actual NDCG harm also requires a score-sensitive downstream mapping and differing labels. Separately trained B+raw and B+symbolic trees have no automatic prediction-fidelity relationship. Audit composed module error, missingness and margin crossings on a fixed chronological warmup audit, then evaluate their separate ranking outcomes only in an authorized fresh reservation.

The minimal engine contract must fix exact topology, parent registry IDs, units/scalers, node identities and export values, product arity, pruning policy, affine ranges, atom order, complexity weight, ties and thresholds. For illustration, width `[[8,0],[3,1],[1,0]]` has four hidden outputs but five incoming subnodes and **44 dense edges**, not 12. Its 1024×512 ceiling gives 524288 row presentations and 23068672 edge-row forward visits before backward work, spline evaluation, audits or grid solves. This is an illustration, not the chosen architecture. Count affine normalizers, additions, products, guards and masks in expanded AST closure; require dimensionless tanh inputs and compatible additive units. Extra missingness columns invalidate the simple two-column storage count unless counted separately.

Freeze a chronological fitting/audit split within the single ≤2000-query/440000-row warmup before checking labels. Clarify whether ≥32 mature positive queries/≥16 positive users are required in fitting, audit, or both; prescribe each support gate rather than sharing one total. An absent support gate ends the branch without shifting the prefix. All labels, scalers, pruning, symbol fits and audit decisions finish by a0; every downstream arm uses the same anchors strictly later. Auditing is one pass with frozen thresholds, not another library/seed/pruning search. Stock per-step test evaluation must not peek at this audit.

There are **five learner attempts plus additional fitting**: one generator, four downstream boosters, up to 36 affine-fit procedures with coefficient reuse, and up to 32 grammar coefficient-fit procedures/1024 coefficient optimizer updates. Each affine procedure has two a/b grid searches and one c/d regression. The stock successful 12-edge/three-atom automation makes up to **48** procedures; the proposed 36 requires retaining and installing winning coefficients without refitting. Specify the custom implementation before accepting that count. Initial spline coefficient construction and any grid refits also belong in the ledger. Invalid/alias grammar proposals consume attempts; the grammar seed, initialization, objective and choice of up to two outputs must be fixed. Equal one-hour caps do not prove equal optimization difficulty.

The published arithmetic is correct: 2032128 bounded grid cells; 0.107666MiB for one buffer; 202.054966GiB for one full-default buffer; 40.565491MiB for two train columns; 147219576 expanded primitive evaluations over supplied train+selection ceilings. These are scoped counts, not peak memory or measured throughput. Six CPU-hours sum correctly but must include every failed fit, grid solve, audit, raw-network score and preprocessing pass. The raw-prefix scanning/state cost is unchanged only if the eight parents are already materialized and authenticated; otherwise separately declare and charge that source work.

**Cheapest next test:** zero-data fixtures for the scale proxy, algebraic/NULL aliases, immutable fit-cache provenance, exact fit counters and composed boundary crossings; no model fitting is needed to expose the present gaps. A later concrete adapter and temporal/support plan may earn a small cost gate. Fresh B41, B+raw, B+symbolic and matched grammar remain necessary controls. If only raw modules improve ranking, reject the symbolic-compression claim. B41 comparisons establish only B41-relative value. To claim improvement over current W, use the same fresh W base in all four downstream arms within the same fit ceiling; this grants no extra fit. Future admission requires fullY conditional macro-query NDCG@20, Recall@20 and paired user-cluster evidence over the matched baseline and grammar under a predeclared decision rule. No such evidence exists here.

## MB3-02 — reject inspected selector; preserve negative evidence

The [ICML2024 paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/bouchiat24a.html) and [Eq17](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.16905v3#S3.SS2.SSS3) use Gaussian posterior parameter MI, −½log(1−Corr²). This measures dependence of scalar output multipliers, not feature-label information, observed exposure or functional nonadditivity. The inspected [interaction callback](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fortuinlab/LA-NAM/main/src/laplace_skorch/callbacks/interaction.py) instead uses unsquared correlation. It zero-fills excluded coordinates with `triu`, passes the whole matrix to top-k, and appends returned pairs. Its auxiliary scalar-multiplier Laplace construction does not pass the main network's learned prior precision; that is a contract difference, not independently proof of another bug. Pair stages train again. [topk_unravel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fortuinlab/LA-NAM/main/src/laplace_skorch/utils.py) confirms whole-matrix flattening without an eligibility filter.

The author's blocking numbers independently reproduce. Covariances with correlation ±0.8 have eigenvalues 0.2/1.8. Eq17 gives 0.510825624 for both; source code gives 0.804718956 and −0.293893332. With D=2,k=1 and negative correlation, the only eligible upper entry is negative and the three excluded entries are zero: top1 must be excluded, irrespective of which zero wins. Squaring alone is insufficient: identity covariance gives all-zero ties, including excluded positions. Select explicit i<j coordinates or invalid −∞ entries, bound k by D(D−1)/2, reject duplicate/self pairs and define stable ties. Near-singular SPD cases additionally need finite scores, bounded factorization residual/jitter and a declared policy; replacing invalid scores silently is not enough.

The rank-one counterexample remains valid after both fixes. With X1=X2=t, a unary true logit t contains no identifiable distinct-parent interaction. P=[[5,4],[4,5]] has eigenvalues **1 and 9** and inverse Σ=(1/9)[[5,−4],[−4,5]], giving correlation −0.8, MI 0.510825624 and precision slack 1.021651248. Equivalently P=I+4·11ᵀ: prior regularization makes the posterior full rank while the observational design remains rank one. Large coupling can arise from redundant unary multipliers. A two-input network does not add a distinct observable on this diagonal support. Require exact/affine alias rejection and supported crossed-input contrasts. The contrast f(a,c)−f(a,d)−f(b,c)+f(b,d) vanishes for additive functions, but fabricating unsupported corners cannot establish an interaction in the available population.

No upstream repair is part of this task. The cheapest gate remains tiny covariance/precision matrices: sign invariance, identity, near singularity, finite nonnegative scores and exactly k eligible distinct stable pairs. MI and marginal-likelihood policies are separate choices, not free fallback attempts. Retain the original failure even if a future pinned revision fixes it.

A repaired future package still has six learner attempts, plus every empirical-Bayes/curvature operation: shared unary, selected/random pair stages, and three matched boosters. Stock second-stage `epochs` is not a 128-minibatch cap. Fix whole-query weights, branch initialization, exact prior/noise assumptions, stage update counters and all main-network versus auxiliary curvature passes; “≤2 streamed passes” must identify which computations it covers. Main-network curvature depends on parameter count, not merely D. The 8937877000 full D41 outer-product cells/820 pairs and 28160000 bounded D8 cells are correct; they omit unary evaluation, main curvature, optimization and scoring. The six-hour/8GiB proposal is unmeasured and grants nothing. A corrected selector must still beat random pairs and existing learned-selection/grammar controls on the same mature later population; posterior MI alone cannot admit it.

**Result:** preserve MB3-02 as a useful negative admission finding. It is not a new accepted model family, and MB3-01's narrower research value does not authorize a generic KAN proposer.
