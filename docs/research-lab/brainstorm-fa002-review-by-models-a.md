## Primary-source verification

[Set-Encoder v5/ECIR2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.06912v5) supports permutation-symmetric interactions between candidate passages using shared interaction states. It also reports that ordinary relevance ranking did not require those interactions, while novelty-specific training exposed their value. The [official repository](https://github.com/webis-de/set-encoder) identifies lightning-ir as its implementation, and the [inspected core](https://github.com/webis-de/lightning-ir/blob/main/lightning_ir/models/cross_encoders/set_encoder.py) appends other-document states with a diagonal self-exclusion mask. It may sample Gaussian states for underfull sets by default. FA05 correctly does **not** copy text models, learned attention, missing-document sampling or empirical gains. A deterministic known-subset max is its own adaptation; category equality does not establish rivalry, substitutes, cannibalization or co-exposure.

[CycleNet v1/NeurIPS2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.18479v1) and [author code](https://github.com/ACAT-SCUT/CycleNet/blob/main/models/CycleNet.py) support learned recurring-cycle lookup by modulo position and residual forecasting. The independent helper inspected the actual trainable table/gather/subtract-and-restore code. FA06 has no learned cycle table or forecaster: its fixed first harmonic is mathematical motivation beyond that source, without borrowed ranking evidence. Mutable author main branches were inspected, not run; a future implementation should pin source references.

The rejection of LiGR as empirical support is correct: its [author arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03417) states withdrawal on8February2026 after discrepancies in claims. Neither proposal needs that withdrawn evidence, external weights, titles, images, impressions or order IDs.

## FA06 — harmonic phase survives the actual baseline and coherence control

The equal-coherence witness passes independently: U1=I=(2−i)/3, U2=(1−2i)/3, so both coherence products are5/9, while alignment is5/9and4/9. The changed middleview remains inside7dand outside24h, with first/latest views unchanged. The actual historical count/distinct/recency/ratio and anchor-calendar fields remain equal. A harmonic clock kernel is **not** ordinary monotone decay with event age. The witness need not preserve a hypothetical event-hour histogram or arbitrary decay sum; those are not in verified W. Adding the full sine/cosine basis and its products would subsume FA06, as the author already states. This prevents an exaggerated novelty claim against every possible temporal feature.

The bounds follow from |U|≤1,|I|≤1and |Re(UconjI)|≤|U||I|. A common origin rotation multiplies both moments by the same unit phasor and cancels. It does not authorize independently shifting users/items or moving physical anchors while pretending window membership is fixed. At positive support, zero resultant is a validzero; missing is determined by N=0only. In floating arithmetic opposite phases leave a tiny residual, so missingness must never depend on exact complexzero. Fix accumulation in a declared precision, final float32 conversion if used by CatBoost, and a predeclared comparator for cancellation/bounds/permutation and the20/100Qdiversity gate. Bounds/counterexample checks were stdlib literals, not native-engine tests.
