## MB01 — replace exponential hard-negative weighting with a bounded pairwise surrogate

**Source fact.** NeurIPS 2024 [PSL](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00163v1) replaces the exponential activation in pairwise-rewritten softmax by alternatives such as `1+tanh(d)`, controlling the relative contribution of high-scoring negatives. Its ranking-bound argument assumes bounded score differences; its CF benchmarks are not daily purchase-set prediction. The [author repository](https://github.com/Tiny-Snow/IR-Benchmark) contains the loss and GPU-oriented tuning examples; those schedules are not our CPU plan.

**Adaptation.** Feed exactly B41's numeric columns and masks to a two-layer width-64 MLP, with score `s=.5*tanh(f(X))`; use that identical bounded scorer in both arms. For each covered positive p, sum over `Nq ∪ {p}`, excluding other positives, then average over positives and informative queries. Arm A uses exponential softmax; arm B uses `L=log sum_j (1+tanh(s_j-s_p))^(1/tau)`, tau fixed at .2. This is a declared multi-positive adaptation; the paper's theorem is not asserted for our full-Y NDCG. Empty/all-positive groups retain evaluation membership but provide no ranking contrast. No sampler or logQ change is needed at C≤220.

**Novelty/dedup.** Existing PairLogit, exact/sampled CE and temperature notes do not replace the activation shaping relative pair gradients. This is not a new negative sampler or an exposure correction. Counterhypothesis: suppressing extremely hard negatives removes precisely the useful scarce purchase signal, or the bounded scorer underfits B41.

**Cheapest falsifier and matched plan.** Zero-fit hand fixtures must show translation invariance, finite gradients, correct masks and a different gradient allocation from exponential loss at the same tau; finite differences check implementation. Then, only with new permission: three attempts total, B41 refit plus the two matched MLPs, one seed, two fixed epochs; 2 threads, total ≤3 CPUh/≤2 wallh, RSS≤8 GiB, streamed query batches≤32. Stop on cap without a refill. Primary contrast is PSL versus matched softmax; either must also challenge B41. All transformations fit on permitted training data; the complete learner must be available before the first evaluation anchor.

## MB02 — parameter-sharing ensemble diversity on the existing tabular inputs

**Source fact.** ICLR 2025 [TabM](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.24210v2) uses shared MLP weights, member-specific multiplicative adapters and separate prediction heads. Individual predictions are supervised independently and averaged at inference. Its benchmark includes classification/regression, mostly A100 training, substantial tuning and many seeds; neither its accuracy nor throughput transfers to this ranking problem. The [official implementation](https://github.com/yandex-research/tabm) is the appropriate source, not a similarly named fork.

**Adaptation.** Use the B41 columns, missing masks and the same train-only numeric transform in all arms. Compare one width-64, two-layer MLP against k=8 TabM with the same trunk width, dropout and per-head purchase loss. Average per-head BCE losses during training; average predicted probabilities before ranking. Query weights sum to one on every mature group, retaining zero-purchase groups. The fixed seed and two epochs are deliberately small; k=8 is an adaptation of the paper's larger ensembles, not an author optimum. At inference a monotonic mean-probability score is fixed before evaluation.

**Novelty/dedup.** Canonical M05 changes candidate-conditioned history interactions; M08 changes representation/learner. Here history and features remain fixed: the intervention is independently trained adapters/heads versus one prediction. Eight purchase heads are not eight event-task heads. A raw member variance is not calibrated uncertainty: shared parameters correlate their errors.

**Falsifier and plan.** First, a source-free fixture checks k=1 reduction, independent head losses and averaging before ranking. Future cost gate: ≤1,000 label-blind sampled training queries, no quality look, comparing forward/backward memory and CPU; this gate is separately paid and not a hidden fit. Full contrast needs three attempts: fresh B41, MLP, k=8 TabM; 2 threads, total≤4 CPUh/≤3 wallh, RSS≤8 GiB, batch≤1,024 rows. A hypothetical dense B41 matrix is about .812 GiB of float32 values; copying it eight times is forbidden. Stream blocks; k expands activations, not stored input. Counterhypothesis: correlated heads add little diversity and spend CPU better used by the single learner. Failure to beat MLP refutes the proposed ensemble mechanism; failure against B41 blocks promotion.

## MB03 — separate teacher imitation from the deployed purchase logit

**Source fact.** RecSys 2024 [Khani et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.14678v1) describe separate hard-label and teacher-label task logits to reduce bias transmission in online ranking, alongside continuously updated teachers and shared soft-label infrastructure. Evidence comes from proprietary multi-objective video systems and months of development; it is not an open REES46 reproduction or a guarantee for purchase ranking.

**Adaptation.** A prefix-trained B41 teacher produces soft purchase scores only on later student-training blocks, after teacher fit completion and label maturity. Student input is B41 X; teacher scores are supervision, never inference features. Compare three identical MLP trunks: purchase-only; direct purchase+teacher loss on one logit; purchase loss on the deployed logit plus teacher loss on a separate head. Fixed lambda=.1 and temperature=1; discard the teacher head at deployment. Teacher uncertainty is not inferred from its confidence, and no impression propensity is estimated.

**Novelty/dedup.** HYP065's purchase+any learns two observed event targets. This tests the routing of one fixed teacher's soft signal while preserving the same true purchase labels and deployed head. It does not become novel merely by adding a head: the required contrast is separate versus direct distillation with identical teacher coverage and trunk capacity.

**Falsifier and plan.** Before fits, a tiny two-head analytic fixture injects a systematically shifted teacher; verify that teacher loss has no direct derivative on the purchase head but still affects the shared trunk. That is a mechanism check, not proof that bias disappears. A future five-attempt ceiling pays for prefix teacher, three students and full-training strong B41; no teacher grid, one final look each, 2 threads, ≤6 CPUh/≤4 wallh, RSS≤8 GiB. Store a single float32 soft score plus keys/masks per candidate row, stream joins, and include teacher inference cost. Counterhypothesis: the early teacher is weaker/staler than the student, and shared-trunk bias persists. If no chronological split leaves adequate purchase support, reject before training. Compare ranking quality and actual inference cost; compression is useful only if quality survives against strong B41 within a predeclared tolerance.

## MB04 — let recommendation gradients improve hierarchical item codes

**Source fact.** SIGIR 2025 [ETEGRec](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.05546v3), first posted in 2024, couples tokenizer and conditional generator through sequence-item and preference-semantic alignment with alternating optimization. Crucially, it uses SASRec collaborative embeddings, so missing item titles do not alone block it. Its Amazon evaluation uses 5-core filtering, per-user leave-one-out and next-item targets. The [author code](https://github.com/RUCAIBox/ETEGRec) expects CUDA PyTorch and offers pretrained SASRec/RQVAE artifacts; none is admissible automatically here.

**Adaptation and novelty.** Fit collaborative item embeddings strictly before downstream anchors, quantize into equal-length hierarchical codes, then compare a frozen-code conditional decoder with the same decoder whose tokenizer receives recommendation alignment updates. Use the same retained timestamp buckets and event types in both. Score only fixed C using complete code log-probabilities, with a deterministic collision suffix; free beam retrieval would be a different experiment. The mechanism is learning the item partition jointly with the decoder, beyond canonical sequence/pooling or graph hops. For horizon purchase sets, average item-code loss over all covered purchased items; this is a new adaptation of next-item training.

**Falsifier, timing and finite plan.** First a zero-fit, hand-constructed code trie checks collision uniqueness, OOV masks, frozen vocabulary and the fact that equal first codes do not force equal final scores. Stop if the intended joint update cannot preserve consistent item↔code↔decoder lineage. A separately approved pilot could pay at most five training jobs: collaborative encoder, initial tokenizer, frozen-code decoder, joint decoder/tokenizer procedure, B41. Two-layer width-64 decoder, history≤20 complete buckets, 2 threads, ≤6 CPUh/≤4 wallh, RSS≤8 GiB; all alternating epochs and failures charged. These caps may simply reject feasibility; no CPU completion claim is made. Vocabulary, learned codes, decoder and actual available_at freeze together before evaluation. Counterhypothesis: one month and sparse purchases cannot support code learning, code collisions dominate tail items, and decoder likelihood improves without full-Y ranking benefit. Defer until MB01/02 or an independently motivated retrieval question justifies this larger cost.

## Additional negative finding and promotion rule

ICDE 2024 [BSL](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.12882v1) was considered but not promoted: its positive log-mean-exp equals the ordinary positive term exactly when a query has one positive. With supplied sparse counts, first establish multi-positive informative support before paying a BSL comparison; grouping users across days to manufacture it changes the task. This mathematical gate is unperformed on actual data.

Promote one mechanism at a time after independent novelty/source review. Report negative, budget-limited and low-support outcomes distinctly. A development improvement, ensemble disagreement or accurate teacher imitation is not evidence of latent preference, calibrated uncertainty, automation transfer, or superiority on another dataset. Cross-dataset claims require a separately reserved dataset, an explicit adapter and the same finite mechanism contrast.
