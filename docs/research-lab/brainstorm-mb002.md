## MB2-01 — exchange information between candidates without depending on their input order

**Source facts.** [Set-Encoder](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.06912v3), first posted2024 and published ECIR2025, shares interaction-token representations between candidate passages while removing passage-order positions. The authors importantly find ordinary relevance ranking can be similarly effective with a pointwise model; gains are clearer for novelty ranking. Its original evidence uses text, ELECTRA initialization and A10040GB training, not purchase data. The [author module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/webis-de/set-encoder/main/set_encoder/module.py) imports its core from `lightning-ir`; the [repository](https://github.com/webis-de/set-encoder) supplies pretrained text models and multiple fine-tuning stages. None is needed or automatically authorized for our numeric adaptation.

**Our mechanism and hypothesis.** Replace independent row scoring by a permutation-equivariant scorer over the **entire existing candidate set**. Encode each B41 row to width32; one two-head attention block exchanges row representations with no candidate-position embedding; a shared head returns one score per candidate. Train with the same query-weighted BCE as controls, including zero-purchase queries. This tests the forward computation, not another listwise loss. Context consists only of other available C rows. It gives the model additional within-query information access even though the per-row columns remain B41; do not call its input information identical to a pointwise scorer.

Other candidates' pair histories may reveal user context that the current candidate's row omits. Counterhypothesis: B41 already represents useful intent, context merely echoes retrieval-source composition, and relational attention overfits sparse purchases. We do not import novelty/diversity targets or presume purchase relevance depends on which candidates were admitted. This is distinct from M05's candidate-conditioned pooling over past events: here the interaction axis is other candidate rows.

**Own literal falsifier, executed without a learner.** Three rows are A=(1,0,0), B=(0,1,0), D=(0,0,h). Let `c=mean(third coordinate)` and `s_i=(x_i1−x_i2)(c−1/6)`. For h=1, A/B scores are +1/6 and−1/6; for h=0 they reverse, although A/B rows themselves are unchanged. All six input permutations preserve keyed scores. Adding five unmasked zero pads reverses the h=1 ordering incorrectly: A=−1/24, B=+1/24. Thus permutation equivariance alone does not establish padding correctness; masking is mandatory. This toy illustrates a realizable computation, not a claim that REES labels follow this rule. A simple mean-context model solves it too, making that control necessary.

**Matched comparison and gates.** Four attempts maximum: fresh frozen-settings B41; rowwise MLP; same MLP with per-query mean/max of the41 columns; candidate-attention model. All have the same missing-value policy and true training labels; controls see explicitly documented information. Query statistics use all real C, never targets. Check empty/shortC, padding, permutation, source-order changes, same-second peers and future suffixes before a fit. Once fit, changing a co-candidate may legitimately change scores; adding synthetic padded rows may not.

One fixed two-epoch schedule, batch≤16Q,2threads, RSS≤8GiB, total≤4CPUh/≤3wallh. At220C, two-head attention logits for16Q occupy approximately5.91MiB float32, but optimizer/activation copies add memory. A26kQ epoch has at most2.5168b head-specific candidate-pair interactions; this is work arithmetic, not a CPU completion forecast. Stop on cap. Numeric transforms and complete learner freeze before evaluation; no fitted context representation is exported to earlier downstream feature anchors. Promotion requires benefit over cheap list statistics and strong B, with unchanged candidate coverage.

## MB2-02 — discover error-amplifying environments, then test their training value

**Source facts.** ICML2024 [XRM](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.16748v2) trains two classifiers on random halves, encouraging each to imitate confident mistakes made on its half by the other. Its [executable algorithm](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/facebookresearch/XRM/main/algorithms.py) keeps dynamic discovery labels, computes cross-mistake annotations from either twin's error against original labels, and class-balances the discovery loss. The [driver](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/facebookresearch/XRM/main/main.py) annotates both train and validation; [dataset code](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/facebookresearch/XRM/main/datasets.py) offers group-balanced sampling. The paper explicitly discusses observational non-identifiability and failure when the easiest learned feature is the invariant one. Image/text subgroup accuracy is not evidence for daily ranking robustness.

**Our adaptation.** Discover training-only environments on B41 X with two small MLP twins, splitting whole users rather than rows. Restore original immutable purchase labels for all downstream training; temporary label flips never become purchase truth. Freeze one discovery setting, with no validation annotation or flip-rate search. Compare the same CatBoost300/depth6/L2=30 procedure under ordinary query weights, class-balanced query weights, XRM class×environment weights, and a within-class seeded shuffle of discovered environment assignments preserving their counts. This uses static group balancing, analogous to the author's RWG path; it is not their complete adaptive GroupDRO algorithm. The intervention is **learned environment assignment**, beyond old fixed query/user weighting. Merely changing row weights is not the novelty claim.

Let base mass be `a_qi=1/|Cq|`; a group's loss uses `Σ a_qi ell / Σ a_qi`, and groups receive equal declared mass. Empty groups stop admission. Class-balanced and shuffled controls separate rare-class emphasis from useful discovered structure. The shuffle preserves class×environment sizes and the exact outer-weight formula. Labels are used only within mature train; discovered m is not an inference feature, since its formula needs y. No group oracle is obtained by observing reserved outcomes.

**Own falsifier.** Author [degeneracy checking](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/facebookresearch/XRM/main/utils.py) requires both environments within each class. The trivial twins-always-negative case makes m=y and is correctly rejected. A stronger literal16-row construction has eight examples of each class; both twins err at indices0,1,8,9, while a real nuisance bit alternates0/1. All four `(y,m)` groups exist with counts6,2,6,2, so the presence check passes; nevertheless the error rate is .25 in both real environments and m reveals none of that environment bit. This does not refute XRM; it refutes interpreting nondegeneracy as environment recovery. A subsequent robust learner can just overweight arbitrary hard/noisy rows.

**Finite plan and disposition.** Six training attempts maximum: two twin networks jointly optimized but separately counted, plus four CatBoost arms. Twins width32/two layers, at most2epochs; no architecture/grid/resume refill.2threads, RSS≤8GiB, total≤6CPUh/≤4wallh, including full training annotation and fitting all controls. Approximately one byte per row can store each binary assignment; probability/logit caches and joins cost extra. Stream rows; do not materialize every transformed copy. Train-only gates report each class×environment support by independent users/days, weight concentration and collapse, with an explicit minimum support set before access. Unknown support currently blocks admission, not research.

This tests an automated weighting package for later full-Y ranking, not causal preference, fairness or universal OOD invariance. Final model availability precedes future scoring; an earlier-anchor FE version would need separate forward snapshots/fits. Defer if discovery just follows class/repeat/support or the shuffled control performs similarly. New time periods, not a worst-group metric chosen after observing them, must decide usefulness.

## MB2-03 — generate local training perturbations conditional on real positive labels

**Source facts and implementation boundary.** ICLR2025 [TabDiff](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/5c882988ce5fac487974ee4f415b96a9-Paper-Conference.pdf) combines numeric and categorical diffusion with feature-specific schedules; its evidence concerns synthetic tables and missing-target imputation. The [author README](https://github.com/MinkaiXu/TabDiff) documents target-from-features imputation, the reverse of our desired feature-from-label augmentation. The generic [sampler](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MinkaiXu/TabDiff/main/tabdiff/models/unified_ctime_diffusion.py) accepts numeric/category masks, allowing a proposed class-clamped missing-feature wrapper. That wrapper is static inference from code, not an author-tested rare-purchase command. Standard target-only guidance is unsuitable for this reverse direction; the first adaptation disables guidance. No foundation checkpoint is required. Paper hardware was RTX A400016GB; CPU capability is not measured CPU feasibility.

**Our hypothesis and controls.** Train a dataset-specific generator only on permitted B41 rows and mature labels. Keep a real parent's query/user context and positive label fixed, mask a predeclared subset of candidate-specific numeric columns, and generate at most four perturbations per real covered positive. This regularizes the same pointwise boosting learner without inserting any synthetic row into real Q/C/Y. Compare original B, mass-matched duplicate-positive augmentation and diffusion augmentation: one generator plus three boosting fits. Divide each original positive's mass among itself and its accepted perturbations; duplicates receive the same number and mass, and total query/class weight remains fixed. Duplication can still affect quantization/stochastic learning, hence its explicit control. Grouped/listwise synthetic data are not proposed.

Counterhypothesis: conditioning preserves a label token but produces unsupported positive feature states, memorizes the few real positives or smooths across a negative region. A feasible feature tuple is not proof of correct synthetic relevance. Since B41 contains redundant counts/ratios/masks, constrain the grammar-derived dependency closure: generate independent primitive fields, recompute known deterministic descendants, and reject broken cross-grain inequalities. Do not silently clip them into validity.

**Own literal rejection gate.** Generated `user_view7d=1, pair_view7d=3` violates pair≤user for the same event/window and cannot represent a real candidate row; the stdlib predicate rejects it. Shared query fields must remain identical across siblings; missing masks and derived ratios must agree. More fundamentally, with identical feature rows and random true winners, adding arbitrarily many synthetic positives cannot create ranking information or independent observations. Retain original user-based uncertainty, never a synthetic-row sample size.

**Cost trap and prospective ceiling.** Author [trainer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MinkaiXu/TabDiff/main/tabdiff/trainer.py) loops over the whole training loader inside `steps`, and [config](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MinkaiXu/TabDiff/main/tabdiff/configs/tabdiff_configs.toml) sets8000. On the supplied5.317m rows with batch4096 this means approximately10.392m gradient updates, plus EMA/full loss passes, not8000updates. Best-checkpoint saving starts after epoch4000. Built-in generation evaluation can train downstream evaluators, so it is not a free diagnostic. A reviewed bounded adapter must disable those jobs, online logging and automatic checkpoint selection.

Propose at most2048 generator updates/batch≤512, a fixed final checkpoint, ≤10,000 total draw attempts,50reverse steps and explicit second-order-call accounting. Four synthetic rows per619full-Y targets bound output by2476; actual covered positives may be fewer.41float32 values for that upper output are .387MiB, excluding metadata and reverse-model activations. One generator≤2CPUh, each of three boosting fits≤1CPUh, preparation/scoring≤1CPUh; total≤6CPUh/≤4wallh,2threads,RSS≤8GiB. If legal accepted rows cannot be produced within the fixed draw cap, stop without refills. This deliberately short generator schedule may fail; no paper-level synthesis quality is promised. Defer actual admission until conditioning, dependency closure and the bounded checkpoint path pass a source-free toy smoke.
