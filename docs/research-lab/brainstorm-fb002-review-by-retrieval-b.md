## FB-05: retain fixed roughness; narrow its attribution

[WEARec v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.07028v2) and its [author code](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xhy963319431/WEARec/master/src/model/wearec.py) operate on sequential item/position embeddings: the implementation decomposes even/odd positions, rescales details and reconstructs them alongside a Fourier branch. The report correctly separates this learned event-index method from its own two-level wall-clock count scalar. The [BSARec reproduction](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.17442v1) supports caution about residual controls and padding in its own setting. It neither predicts our gain nor licenses filling unavailable history with zeros.

The printed four-bin example establishes different E1 under matching stated totals and recencies; it does not explicitly match a decay. A stronger literal fixture now does. At ages `(6d,4d,2d,1h)`, take A counts `(3,0,2,1)` and B `(1,3,1,1)`. Both have N6, C24h1, C7d6 and first/last ages6d/1h. Consequently the corresponding recent-versus-older count trend also matches. Those event ages occupy bins4,13,22,31. Both E1/E2 numerators are14 in A versus12 in B, hence normalized values7/18 versus1/3.

No duplicate rows are needed: A assigns `{x,y,z};{};{x,y};{x}` and B `{x};{x,y,z};{y};{x}` across those four timestamps. Both retain product totals x3/y2/z1 and distinct support3. Use an unchanged, unviewed candidate in the common category. This proves nonalias to that category count/decay/control definition; it does not prove all B41 fields or candidate-item decay are identical. Nor does it distinguish the energies from all32 exact fine counts: they are deterministic functions of that histogram. Claim a compact nonlinear summary under a limited column budget, not new information beyond the complete histogram or all possible decay grids.

For nonnegative counts and N>0, each energy lies in[0,1]. Multiplying every count by a common factor leaves it unchanged, so uniform duplicate replication cannot explain a change; uneven repeated logging can. One event yields E1=E2=1 wherever it falls, exposing a strong sparse-support effect. A flat32-bin histogram yields0. Under orthonormal Haar scaling the corresponding normalized coefficient energies are E1/2 and E2/4. “Haar-like roughness” is accurate; these are not percentages of total energy or evidence of daily/weekly periodicity.

Freeze bins as `[t−604800+j×18900,t−604800+(j+1)×18900)`, j0…31. The left endpoint is included; t is excluded. One-second crossing can sharply change adjacent-pair energies, while translating all events and anchor together preserves the representation. The phase/jitter diagnostic must therefore be prespecified and must not become a search over phases, time grids, repeated rows or jitter realizations after inspecting quality. A coarse calendar histogram cannot exactly reproduce arbitrary anchor-relative boundaries.

The count-contrast arm needs the same source-coverage, N>0 and candidate-metadata masks. Otherwise a difference can arise from unavailable history rather than roughness. Complete observed silence and incomplete source coverage may both yield missing energies, but their existing support reasons stay distinct. Use all raw view multiplicity consistently with the count control, or name deduplication as a separate adaptation. No need to add an uncharged spectral encoder, fitted filter, extra window grid or dispersion column.

## FB-06: retain the profile-level center with explicit session semantics

[GROS v1, equations1/7](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.15442v1) minimizes the radius covering a strict majority and permits centers among the observed estimators. Its concentration claims assume independent group estimators; five recent shopping prefixes do not satisfy that condition merely because they have different session IDs. No official implementation was found in the bounded paper/publisher/source search, so this review checks the mathematical objective rather than claims author-code equivalence.

The [HyPro paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41019-025-00301-1) reports that denoising can hurt short sessions and proposes learned removal/enhancement channels. Its [official repository](https://github.com/jarviswww/Code4HyPro) was located, but the model source fetch failed. That warning is supported by the paper; neither HyPro's learner nor performance is imported into this five-profile statistic.

With self-inclusive third-neighbor radius, the original nonalias works: `[1,1,1,0,0]` and five`.6` profiles both have mean`.6`; using five events per session gives identical pooled A/B counts15/10. Their selected A affinities are1 versus`.6`. Thus event-pooled counts, equal-session means and support alone do not determine the center. For binary categories TV is absolute difference, so this example is also a median-like special case; it is not proof of a distinct general-purpose robust learner. For multiple categories, choose the entire profile before candidate projection, not separate coordinate medians that could describe no selected session.

Key sessions by `(user_id,user_session)`. Skip missing session IDs rather than merging them into one fictitious session. Define the last **past view** timestamp used for recency, select only nonempty in-window prefixes, and never inspect later tails or inferred completion. Current-session past views remain usable; its future tail is not. Histories crossing the left boundary use their in-window views exactly as stated. Session recency ties and center-radius ties both need a deterministic final rule independent of source row order. A technical session-ID tie is possible if declared; it establishes no event chronology. Alternatively mark an unresolved five-session boundary unavailable identically in both arms.

Unknown historical category may occupy an explicit profile mass bucket so TV remains defined. It must not make an unknown **candidate** category into an affinity match. Canonical metadata resolves the complete latest global bucket<t; a known value plus NULL or conflicting values is ambiguous, with no older backoff. In that case conditional candidate affinity/energy stays NULL. Preserve the row and mask, rather than inner-joining it away or replacing unknown with zero. Historical categories remain those of their individual events.

The mean control correctly uses the same five prefixes and the same fewer-than-five mask; comparing against event-weighted means would confound robustness and session weighting. Keep the pooled-count baseline as an additional diagnostic. Negative controls should include four sessions, five one-view sessions, all identical profiles, recent minority interest, and relabeling/reordering tied peers. A rise confined to the common session-count/missing flag does not establish robust aggregation. Choosing the majority can systematically suppress newly emerging intent, so short-history and shifting-category slices remain necessary.

## FB-07: keep deferred and isolate information

The [Human–LLM feature-engineering paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.21060v1) uses variational BNN uncertainty, text-embedding-3-small plus column-usage encoding, UCB, downstream learner evaluation and optional pairwise human feedback. Exact linear-Gaussian inference over local typed AST tokens is the proposal's adaptation. The paper names the OpenAI embedding model; actual API batching, calls and cache behavior were not verified from code. No official implementation was found in the bounded search. Neither its confidence lemma nor its throughput transfers to the custom surrogate/proxy.

The literal exploration behavior passes: with prior covarianceI and observation variance1, six zero-valued probes at `(1,0)` give mean0 and covariance `diag(1/7,1)`. Equal-mean candidates on coordinates1/2 therefore have different uncertainty; positive fixed UCB selects2, while a declared greedy tie can select1. Giving2 proxy gain+1 or−1 supplies benefit/harm worlds. This is posterior algebra, not calibration or real proxy evidence. Descriptor collisions retain distinct AST identities even if the surrogate cannot distinguish their predictions.

Six initial observations plus two opportunities per arm are **16 logical policy exposures**. With six physically shared initial evaluations, at most10 unique physical proxy evaluations are needed before failures/retries; overlapping later requests can reduce that number. Count both ledgers. Cache hits and failed requests consume their arm's opportunity without refill. Freeze the common six-score information set; one arm must not read the other's later scores before choosing its own request. Physical reuse is allowed after overlapping requests are fixed. A globally visible result cache otherwise creates unequal feedback histories.

Before source work, freeze proxy definition and conditioning, mature label cut, fitted bins, prior/noise, descriptor scaling, UCB multiplier/ties, AST legality/dedup/closure rules and failure handling. Cache identity binds exact typed AST, source/anchor/label cuts, proxy and bin versions, NULL/closure semantics and seed. “Fit-free proxy” can still be label-bearing and statistically selected; its mining and evaluation cost is not zero. Selection must finish before later application, and inspected development remains screening evidence.
