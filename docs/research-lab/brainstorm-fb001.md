## FB-01 — second-order action-path area

**Source fact.** Walker et al., ICML2024, construct Log-NCDEs using a truncated log-signature and Lie brackets. Section2.4 defines iterated integrals, explains redundancy of signature coordinates, and motivates logarithmic coordinates. Their evaluated learner is a differential-equation neural model on multivariate time series; the paper does not evaluate ecommerce counts or this feature. [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/walker24a.html), [mathematical definition](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18512v3#S2.SS4).

**Adaptation.** For candidate pair `(u,i)`, represent each strict-past second in a 7d window by its vector of view/cart/purchase multiplicities. Add three antisymmetric areas, one for each action pair: `A_ab = Σ_{s<r}(n_a(s)n_b(r) − n_b(s)n_a(r))`. This is twice the corresponding degree-two log-signature coordinate for a piecewise-linear cumulative-count path. Simultaneous actions contribute no invented ordering. Emit raw signed areas, with existing counts/coverage available to the tree. No trained encoder or arbitrary item embedding is needed.

**Novelty delta.** Counts are first-order totals; areas distinguish cross-action order. This is not pooled category-transition association NB-F03, ItemCF, recency, a rate or a renamed grammar bundle. Literal witness: sequences `VVCCVPC` and `VCVCVPC` at seconds1–7 have identical action counts and latest view/purchase/cart times. Their `A_VC` values are5 and3. All events can concern the same user/item/category/price. This establishes a functional nonalias, not predictive utility.

**Counterhypothesis / cheapest falsifier.** Areas primarily encode length or repeated logging and add no useful signal once counts and recencies are present. First use that seven-event witness, a simultaneous `V+C` bucket, and future-suffix invariance: failure rejects the definition before data. A future matched quality comparison is B versus B+three areas, with an additional prespecified order-shuffled history control preserving bucket counts and final action times. Shuffling is a diagnostic, not a new training population or proof of causality.

**Cost / availability.** Three physical columns; no fitted state. Chronological concatenation is associative but noncommutative; exact rolling expiry needs a queue/aggregate structure, not subtracting ordinary cumulative areas. For a later 100-query/≤22,000-pair pilot, cap matched history at1M records, source work at30 CPU minutes, peak RSS4GB; exceeding any cap gives STOP without incomplete features. Two matched downstream fits require their own≤90 CPU-minute reservation. Source metadata and global peer policy remain strict `<t`. Claimed ranking gain and throughput: unmeasured.

## FB-02 — item audience contribution shape

**Source fact.** Mayfly, December2024, separates per-device/window aggregation and contribution bounding from later cross-device summation and noise. Its privacy unit and completed-window availability are explicit. This is a federated analytics system, not a recommendation feature study. [Primary paper, §§3.1–3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.07962v1#S3.SS1).

**Adaptation.** For each candidate item, let `n_v` be each viewer's number of views during `[t−7d,t)`. Add `cap2=Σ_v min(n_v,2)`, concentration `h=Σ_v n_v²/(Σ_v n_v)²`, and `maxshare=max_v n_v/Σ_v n_v`. At zero total, `cap2=0`, ratios missing; existing item-view count/availability supplies the denominator support and mask if actually identical at this grain/window. Otherwise physical closure must charge the extra support/mask before allocation. These are diagnostics supplied alongside raw history; no rows are removed. No DP or calibrated uncertainty guarantee is claimed.

**Novelty delta.** Raw total and distinct audience are endpoints of a contribution-cap curve, but do not determine its interior or concentration. Two audiences `(5,1)` and `(3,3)` have six views and two viewers; `cap2` is3 versus4, concentration26/36 versus18/36, maxshare5/6 versus1/2. This differs from duplicate fraction, user-category entropy, shrinkage and HYP069/070 audience-neighbor retrieval: only the shape of a candidate's contributing audience changes.

**Counterhypothesis / cheapest falsifier.** A loyal small audience may be genuinely predictive; concentration can penalize valid repeat demand, and raw count/distinct interactions may already approximate everything needed. Check the two literal histograms and expiry of one viewer's event first. Then B versus B+the closure above on identical Q/C/Y, settings and iteration prefix. Prespecify low-distinct and repeat-target slices; a pooled gain driven solely by known-pair purchase behavior does not establish robust new-pair popularity.

**Cost / availability.** Exact per-item/viewer multiplicity, square-sum updates and an exact maximum structure are required. A future 100-query pilot caps distinct matched `(item,viewer)` keys and matched events at1M each,30 CPU minutes,4GB peak RSS, three columns only if old supports truly match. Additional masks reduce the core allocation or reject this bundle. Fixed cap2 has no fitted threshold. Same-second additions occur after all queries at that second; source coverage is retained. Two quality fits≤90 CPU minutes are proposed, not authorized. Reliability interpretation and effect are unmeasured.

## FB-03 — purchase-reset observed action episode

**Source fact.** Kourani, Park and van der Aalst's September2025 POWL preprint derives strict partial orders, preserves repeated activities and abstracts loops. Atomic events can remain unordered when timestamps coincide. Its discovery guarantees concern process-model soundness/fitness, not inference of an actual shopping cart. The accessed v1 explicitly identifies itself as not peer reviewed. [Primary preprint, §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.15346v1).

**Adaptation.** Define `p` as the last observed purchase second of `(u,i)` before t. Within the7d history, emit views and carts strictly after `p`, plus carts in the purchase peer bucket. With no observed purchase, post-reset values are missing, using the existing pair-purchase availability mask if its semantics match. The third column records ordering ambiguity rather than placing same-second carts before/after purchase. This is an observed episode, not unfinished inventory: purchase quantities, cart removals and orders are unavailable.

**Novelty delta.** A target-specific reset changes the history boundary rather than selecting another fixed window. With purchase at4, latest cart7 and latest view8, cart times `(1,2,7)` versus `(1,6,7)` have the same three carts, action recencies and supports; post-purchase carts are1 versus2. This differs from NB-F01 context recurrence before a previous candidate purchase, NB-F02 renewal waiting time and FB-01 cumulative cross-action area. Reset episodes intentionally discard pre-reset evidence; areas retain order across it.

**Counterhypothesis / cheapest falsifier.** Purchase may not reset intent, and repeated purchases or delayed logs can make this arbitrary segmentation harmful. Literal checks: the two sequences; cart/purchase peers; no prior purchase; and a purchase just outside7d. A future comparison matches B+three reset columns against B+three fixed-window action columns at the same feature/training budget, then checks both against B. If reset only wins because the comparator omitted an availability flag, reject the interpretation.

**Cost / availability.** Three cores, with masks reused only after exact closure review. Latest-purchase state plus bounded recent action queues; no fitted state or learned process miner. Same100-query/1M matched-event/30 CPU-minute/4GB source ceiling;≤90 CPU minutes for two separately reserved fits. The previous purchase can precede the feature window, so read it from observed prefix; October's left boundary cannot establish lifetime absence. Unknown ingestion delay remains the existing replay assumption. Quality and useful episode frequency remain unmeasured.

## FB-04 — transfer a feature-arm router across support contexts

**Source fact.** Pedersen and Crandall, ICMLA2025, transfer assumption-checker vectors with AATention, then train generator-performance predictors in the target domain. Their AlegAAATr uses per-generator KNN; transfer did not eliminate target-domain learning and struggled with cooperative patterns in one evaluation domain. [Author-hosted paper, §§III–VI](https://faculty.cs.byu.edu/~crandall/papers/Pedersen_ICMLA1_2025.pdf).

**Adaptation / delta.** In a separate future development reservation, keep fixed trained B and feature-arm models, but learn which arm to use for a whole query from three existing past-only query-support descriptors: observed user-history age, viewed-item breadth and fraction of C with prior pair history. This routes a ranking model; it does not average uncalibrated candidate scores. Train on mature query-level paired NDCG differences, with user-grouped forward folds. Hold one prespecified support quadrant out from router fitting to test transfer across query contexts. This is neither HYP014's unavailable external pretrained meta-ranker nor NB-F04's label-free temporal-stability allocation: it changes from one global winning arm to support-conditional inference.

**Counterhypothesis / falsification.** Apparent specialization is noise from sparse positives, and a router overfits more than the best global arm. Cheapest first check is algebraic: if every arm has the same query ordering, any router has zero achievable gain. This requires approved development predictions later, not heldout inspection now. Next compare transferred router, best global arm, matched random routing and a simple support-rule router; score only a fresh explicitly reserved period. Reusing old selection scores is screening, not confirmation.

**Finite feasibility.** At most five fixed arms; no additional downstream fits if reusable approved predictions exist, but one router fit and all new label looks/scoring are charged. Proposed router: KNN k15 over≤2,000 mature labelled query summaries, three inputs,≤10MB numeric storage; total additional work≤30 CPU minutes/4GB RSS, then STOP. Online first routes, then scores one arm; batch reuse may instead need all arms and must charge that computation. Models and router must exist before their first evaluation query, with target rewards matured before router fit. No trained router, sufficient support, or transfer gain currently exists. This candidate is deferred pending prediction-access and support gates.
