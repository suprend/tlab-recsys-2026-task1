# Transfer A / round 001 — learn reusable search behavior, not column-name portability

15 October 2026. Independent first draft, frozen before reading other NEW-IDEA reports. Temporary candidate IDs TA-01–03 are not canonical HYP IDs. Scope: primary papers/author repositories and existing canonical documentation only. No actual source data, database, query labels, models, paid input hashes, fits, inference, native APIs, downloads, or experimental jobs were accessed. Current actual015/016, winner R2, legal pool and reservations remain unchanged.

Canonical dedup: HYP002/011 already cover bounded typed feature search and nested references; HYP012 covers a semantic/blinded proposer; HYP014 rejected an unavailable ExploreKit meta-ranker. Rounds 011/013/016/018/028/040/052/068/070/073/074 already cover roles, grains, multiplicity, conservative equality, conformance and cache/vocabulary identity. None of the candidates below claims novelty for adding those checks. Their proposed novelty is **how a future search learns which complete legal program to examine**. These are hypotheses inferred from the papers, not reported ecommerce results.

REES observables are event time, view/cart/purchase, user/product/session IDs, category ID/path, brand and price. No impression, title, image, order ID or reliable ingestion clock is invented. For every future study, history uses strict event-time `<T` under an explicitly declared offline availability assumption; truly known delayed fields require their separate availability clock. Source-task outcomes must mature before the transfer artifact is frozen at M; M precedes any destination outcome opening. Destination target, Q, C, metric, missingness and coverage contracts remain local.

## TA-01 — induce typed macros from source programs, then transfer the search prior

**Primary mechanism.** [LILO, ICLR 2024](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/819cebb05f993840e8a52d7564c5c282-Abstract-Conference.html) alternates synthesis, symbolic library compression and documentation; its evaluation concerns string, scene and graphics programs. The [author repository](https://github.com/gabegrand/lilo) releases the implementation. The transferable idea is compression-derived reusable abstractions. Our initial experiment omits the LLM/AutoDoc stage and tests that smaller mechanism explicitly; it does not reproduce the full LILO result.

**New experiment.** Learn at most two parameterized macros from source-only feature ASTs by anti-unifying recurring typed subtrees. An example shape is “user's distinct past items → item-level past statistic → user reference,” with action, window and statistic as typed arguments. Freeze macro definitions and proposal probabilities at M. Destination search expands these macros into the existing primitive language before legality, closure, cost and fitting. Unlike HYP002's prewritten families or shared execution DAG, source solutions change the proposal distribution over previously unseen compositions; physical caching is irrelevant.

**Observables and clocks.** Initial inputs are source program definitions, declared roles/grains and source-free literal specifications, not destination Y. An optional later utility-weighted macro corpus may contain only source selection outcomes already mature by M. Category and price references remain as-of; a macro cannot manufacture a missing cart channel or infer invoice grain from matching names.

**What transfers / fails.** Transfer argument structure and a search prior, never IDs, learned weights, absolute popularity values or source-specific 24h utility. An unavailable role rejects a binding. Embedded category and property-state versions require different legal resolvers. A compressed macro's short spelling does not make its expanded program statistically simpler.

**Counterhypothesis and tiny falsification.** Compression merely gives a handpicked source pattern an unfair complexity discount. Use two source schemas and one renamed/repartitioned destination schema, 24 source ASTs, at most 32 literal event/property rows and six held-out composition specifications. Compare macro-biased and flat enumeration with the same expanded-AST size ceiling, primitive set and 512 complete-candidate evaluations each; charge induction CPU. A shuffled-source-subtree prior is a negative control. Reject the mechanism if it solves no additional held-out specifications before the fixed cap, or only wins when expanded size is hidden. Publish invalid attempts without refill.

**Finite budget and meaning.** Prospective ceiling: three schemas, two macros, ten CPU minutes, 512 MiB, zero model fits. Primary outcome is held-out specification solve count and paid search CPU. Exact feature reconstruction is not purchase-ranking quality or cross-dataset NDCG. Only a separately reserved subsequent two-fit matched comparison could examine predictive utility; no present budget follows.

## TA-02 — transfer edits to programs, rather than restarting feature synthesis

**Primary mechanism.** [Rule et al., Nature Communications 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-50966-x) represent metaprograms that revise programs and evaluate learning of list-processing rules. The [author-hosted paper](https://colala.berkeley.edu/papers/rule2024symbolic.pdf) reports ablations of program and metaprogram priors. It motivates structured edits; it supplies no ecommerce feature-transfer evidence.

**New experiment.** Learn a small distribution over typed edits from source feature-specification pairs: replace action/window; substitute count with distinct count; change pair grain to category-conditioned grain when declared membership exists; add an explicit reference normalization. On a destination schema, map source roles, seed with an applicable complete program and search over at most two edits. The resulting programs must pass the usual independent literals and conformance gates. Those gates prune search immediately, so failures alter the next legal proposal rather than merely decorating an audit report.

This differs from TA-01: the primitive language and macro inventory stay fixed; the learned object is a transition between whole hypotheses. It differs from HYP002 and conservative rewrites: these edits intentionally change feature values and semantics, rather than claiming equivalence. It differs from HYP012: no external semantic proposer receives feedback.

**Observables and clocks.** Source examples are explicit input/output feature specifications with source schema annotations, frozen by M. Destination examples are source-free, independently authored desired feature values, not purchase outcomes. They are disclosed equally to every control. Any later usefulness ranking must be source-only and frozen; adapting an edit after destination Y would be a different experiment. Session-scoped edits require declared session namespace and cannot treat session as order.

**Transfer limits.** Reuse “which structured change often repairs this role mismatch,” not a claim that views and purchases share target semantics. When a channel, grouping key or membership clock is missing, reject that edit. A source count cannot automatically become purchase quantity or invoice count. Typed admissibility alone does not establish usefulness.

**Counterhypothesis and tiny falsification.** Source seeds merely put the desired answer close to the start. Predeclare six destination tasks: two one-edit, two two-edit and two deliberately distant specifications. Give the same seeds to a control that selects edits uniformly, and give a scratch enumerator equal complete-program evaluations. Use one held-out edit composition and reverse source/destination roles. If the learned edit prior only wins on copied tasks, loses to uniform edits, or exhausts its budget on distant tasks, reject transfer rather than adding restarts.

**Finite budget and meaning.** Two source plus two destination schemas; no more than eight edit rules, 32 literal rows, 256 evaluated complete ASTs per arm, ten CPU minutes total, 512 MiB, zero fits. Primary outcome: exact generalization on undisclosed literal specifications, with solved count and CPU. This is program adaptation, not predictive generalization. A later quality experiment would require its own destination purchase protocol and matched fitting cost; list-rule acquisition rates and purchase NDCG are non-equivalent.

## TA-03 — source-only routing between fixed feature families, with an explicit identifiability test

**Primary mechanism and boundary.** [Relatron, ICLR 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.22552v1) learns task-aware routing between DFS and relational deep learning using a model-performance bank. Its homophily uses labels; affinity includes fitted linear heads and TabPFN validation. Thus its published method is **not** a label-free destination selector. The [author README](https://github.com/amazon-science/Automating-Relational-Machine-Learning) says the precomputed bank requires contacting the authors. We neither possess that bank nor count it as a free input.

**New experiment.** Test the deliberately weaker source-only variant before attempting expensive architecture routing. Route between two fixed symbolic bundles: direct pair/repeat features and category/item-context features. Learn a tiny router from a bounded source bank, using declared target type/horizon plus label-free past descriptors: capability vector, history length, user/item degree concentration, repeat fraction, category coverage and pair sparsity. The destination supplies these descriptors before M but no Y or validation score. Return one bundle or abstain to a frozen default; no destination search follows.

**Affordable source bank.** [PluRel, ICML 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.04029v2) separates synthetic schema generation, FK connectivity and conditional feature mechanisms; the [author code](https://github.com/stanford-star/plurel) exposes these controls. Our proposed tiny event generator borrows that separation, not its billion-token pretraining or published performance. Predeclare three synthetic source mechanisms: repeat-dominant, category-homophilic and popularity-dominant. Each has 128 mature source queries, at most 2,048 past events and two fixed bundle fits. Source outcomes are generated locally and mature before M. Public real datasets remain metadata-only candidates; no download is needed for this first falsifier.

**Counterhypothesis / fastest stop.** Unlabelled descriptors cannot reveal which signal determines purchases. Create an indistinguishable descriptor pair with opposite synthetic outcome mechanisms: repeat versus category. A universal successful router is impossible there. Predeclare this as the abstention test, not a surprising failure discovered after fitting. Then test a distinct held-out schema/mechanism combination. Compare source-conditioned routing with the globally best source bundle and a seeded random route, all using the same bank and one fitted model per chosen destination arm. Reject if regret against the best destination arm does not improve on the global default, or abstention fails on the indistinguishable pair.

**Clocks, transfer and budget.** Transfer the family-selection policy only. Descriptors use authorized prefixes, fixed domains and past-only metadata; no full-file ID universe or future coverage estimate. Prospective ceiling: four schemas including destination, 6 source fits, 3 destination fits, 1 router fit, 30 CPU minutes, 1 GiB, two bundles and one predefined look per task/arm. Count failures and descriptors in CPU; no hyperparameter sweep. Synthetic query-ranking regret is the first metric. Synthetic labels, Relatron AUROC and real daily full-purchase-Y NDCG are separate estimands. Success would justify a new real-source reservation, not a current016 improvement claim.

## Decision boundary

TA-01 and TA-02 can first fail with zero predictive fits; TA-03 should remain conditional until its label-free identifiability/abstention test survives. As a separate negative finding, [Relational Transformer](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06377v2) explicitly lists recommendation/link prediction as unsupported and relies strongly on past task-table labels. Its zero-shot headline cannot serve as a drop-in REES ranking baseline. The three proposed mechanisms can share existing legality checks, but their budgets are alternatives for review, not additive grants or reasons to reopen actual015/016.
