## Exact bound; exact limits of the stopping claim

Primary remains **conditional macro-positive-query fullY NDCG@20**, not the all-query zero-convention metric. For the same mature cohort let Q+ contain every query with nonempty fullY, including positive queries with empty C or all targets outside C. Write `m_q=|Y_q|`, `h_q=|Y_q∩C_q|`, `D(r)=Σ_(j=1…r)1/log2(j+1)` and

`O_q=D(min(20,h_q))/D(min(20,m_q)); H=mean_(q∈Q+)(O_q−W_q)`.

Every reranking confined to this C has point improvement over frozen tuned W at most H on this exact cohort. Thus `H<δ` prevents meeting the point condition `Δ≥δ` there. With the historical practical δ=.002, equality H=.002 does **not** justify stopping. An implementation must use a conservative numerical upper bound, not rounded display scalars. Empty Q+ makes H undefined, not zero or evidence of a perfect baseline. Do not substitute hit-rate, micro target coverage or 1−W for this bound.

This is finite-sample arithmetic, **not** population futility, a statistical CI, achievable learnability or permission to open another cohort. It does not show an AST could attain O, and H>δ does not authorize extra fits. The oracle needs actual per-query m/h and W rankings; published 65/96 coverage cannot reconstruct it. Computing a fresh authorized oracle/support result is target-dependent feedback even at zero model fits. No such computation occurred here; no current heldout or016 budget is reused.

## Four literal falsifiers

**1. Wrong rare-event denominator.** A cohort has 1000 mature queries and one positive query whose headroom is .1. The all-query-zero headroom is .0001, below .002; primary conditional headroom is .1. Stopping from .0001 answers a different question and would reject a potentially large conditional improvement. More zeroY queries do not manufacture positive ranking evidence.

**2. Invented ranking opportunity.** For one positive query m=2,h=1, with W already ranking the only retrievable positive first, `O=W=1/(1+1/log2(3))≈.613147192765`, so H=0. The shortcut 1−W≈.386853 falsely counts an off-C target as reranking headroom. These numbers and the strict-threshold equality boundary were checked using only invented arithmetic.

**3. Repeated buyers are not independent new buyers.** Twenty positive queries from one user provide one positive-user cluster, not twenty. In a literal 100-mature-user population with only that one positive user, uniform bootstrap sampling 100 users with replacement has zero-positive-draw probability `(99/100)^100≈.366032`. Conditional metric draws with no positive query stay undefined; resampling until defined changes the procedure. More repetitions improve Monte Carlo precision, not the number of observed positive users. The same observed query mean can coexist with very different user concentration and future populations. Counts alone do not establish power; any minimum-support threshold is an engineering feasibility rule with declared rationale, not a universal significance guarantee.

**4. A cheap ordering cannot identify a late winner.** Early scores are A=.60,B=.40 in two invented worlds. Full-budget scores can be (.61,.80) or (.80,.41); both trajectories are monotone, yet their winners differ. Halving B after the early score cannot distinguish these worlds. Additional fidelity evidence could be useful, but obtaining it consumes resource and feedback. This is an allocation falsifier, not proof that early stopping always fails. Existing trained 300/checkpoint scoring is not a paid low-fidelity stopping experiment when 300 trees were already trained before the first look.

## Simplest prospective control and honest outcome accounting

First use a zero-fit literal controller replay: fixed candidate IDs, the two fidelity worlds, a metric disclosed before an integrity failure, and a failure before feedback. Verify exact transitions, consumed opportunities and fallbackW. The output is scheduler correctness, not quality. One deliberately small proposal permits ≤ 8 literal records, ≤ 32 transitions and zero actual learning/prediction; no source job is needed.

A later **separately reserved** direct-quality comparison can be narrower than a learned bandit. Policy S fixes two AST IDs/order by a declared seed before outcomes. Policy A shares the first ID and, after its first allowed result, chooses the second ID from the same frozen legal bank ≤ 32 programs. Both get exactly two nonrefillable fit opportunities and two candidate-utility disclosures, with the same tuned W, full fidelity, scoring prefix, source prefix, Q/C/fullY, weights, learner and winner/tie rule. This narrow design allows zero additional proposer/surrogate fits: its four learning-invocation credits are already allocated to candidate attempts. A policy requiring another learned fit needs an explicitly redesigned future reservation or is deferred; calling it optimizer overhead does not exempt it. Fixed order remains a legitimate baseline.

The shared oracle/support gate is one target-dependent opening, delivered identically to both policies. Globally reserve ≤ 4 native candidate-fit credits and ≤ 5 computed search-feedback payloads: one gate plus four candidate results. Per-policy information receipts are ≤ 3, so six credits across policies if the shared gate is conservatively charged twice. Reusing an exactly identical first-fit receipt can reduce actual physical invocations to 3; the four allocated opportunities remain consumed and actual/credited counts are reported separately. A payload is a predeclared bounded NDCG/Recall/cost/status vector; arbitrary extra checkpoints, slices, thresholds or source-level target tables are not free feedback.

Every allocated slot logs AST/source identity, attempted/started/completed fit, CPU/wall/RSS, fidelity, metric disclosed and eligibility. Failure before native start has zero actual fit starts but consumes its opportunity and preparation. Failure before any utility has zero ranking looks; failure after a disclosed checkpoint consumes both the attempt and that look, and an ineligible partial model cannot win. Duplicate/invalid ASTs and stopped/censored trials do not trigger replacement. Saved runtime does not buy another seed, configuration, checkpoint or look.

Choose among valid complete candidates and W by a frozen rule, allowing W to remain winner; do not require a successful new feature. Selected full-budget model receipts are reused later rather than pretending their fits were only probes. If six learner-based proxy probes and two new final fits were substituted, there would be eight training invocations, not two. Fit-free feedback and downstream ranking are different estimands; improvement of one does not certify the other.
