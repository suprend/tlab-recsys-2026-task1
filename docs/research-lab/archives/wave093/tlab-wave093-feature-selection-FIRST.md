**Mechanism / prediction.** For a deterministic transformation g(S) whose complete operands S are retained, `Y ⟂ g(S) | S` and `I(Y;g(S)|S)=0`, regardless of its usefulness to a restricted learner. Conditional resampling of g given S is a point mass and cannot produce a distinct shadow. Therefore a universal “zero conditional information ⇒ useless representation” rule must reject some useful finite-class transformations. This does not apply to a generated aggregate not recoverable from the retained supports; current eight bundles are not claimed deterministic functions of B41.
