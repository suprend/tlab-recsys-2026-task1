# Wave026 FIRST B — buy precision, not more apparent positives

Independent FIRST; peer026 unread. One optional precision-design refinement under existing TLAB013/015 and rounds005/019; zero new HYPs. Current opened final is design history, never a new independent test. No present models/features are changed;015/018/report do not depend on this proposal.

**Mechanism — frozen past-only stratified hash sampling.** Before a genuinely future evaluation period, declare two strata from strict-past context, such as cart-present/absent. Freeze allocation using a separately declared mature pilot’s paired-contrast dispersion and preparation/scoring costs, not purchase frequency alone. Every nonempty stratum retains positive inclusion probability. Within strata use bottom-hash with an independently precommitted random salt; record exact inclusion probabilities. No request is selected using its future purchase.

**Estimand/control.** For conditional query-macro contrast, estimate `sum(Ipositive·delta/pi)/sum(Ipositive/pi)`; retain fullY and fixedC. Horvitz–Thompson totals are design-unbiased under valid sampling; their ratio is not generally finite-sample unbiased. Uncertainty must respect sampling design, repeated users and observed calendar scope; ordinary unweighted bootstrap is not automatically valid. Strong control: unchanged fixed hash-cohort on the same eligible population, frozen B/W and total physical budget. No favourable seed selection, different retrieval or extra model tuning.

**Prediction/falsifier.** If past strata predict paired-ratio influence dispersion sufficiently to offset preprocessing, allocation may reduce estimator MSE per paid CPU. More positives alone is insufficient. Homogeneous/shifted dispersion, large inverse weights, zero observed positive denominator or preprocessing overhead can erase the benefit. Reject the efficiency claim if same-budget hash control ties/beats MSE or interval calibration fails; narrower biased intervals are not success.

**Counterexample, hypothetical only.** Population fractions H/L=.1/.9; purchase probabilities=.1/.01; conditional paired deltas=+.1/−.2. Population contrast is−.0421. Sampling H/L equally and reporting an unweighted conditional mean gives+.0727. Past-only selection still changes the estimand without weights. Repeating the same users or increasing bootstrap draws creates no new independent outcome information.

This checks estimator/sampler logic only; actual prospective benefit requires separately authorized new-period evidence.
