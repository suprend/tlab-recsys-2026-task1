## Four explicit fields

**Mechanism — MB9-R01.** Fit one small purchase-supervised proxy over B41 plus a fixed pool of legal scalar AST outputs. Train view/cart readouts on its detached hidden representation. Auxiliary gradients never update the shared trunk. At eight predetermined training checkpoints, differentiate each loss through hypothetical feature gates solely to compute proposal credits. Add a bounded auxiliary credit only when its gate-gradient vector has positive cosine with the purchase vector. Freeze one bundle from this credit and one from purchase credit alone. Separately train the fixed purchase-only boosting learner on each bundle.

**Testable prediction.** With the same purchase proxy trajectory, pool, bundle size and final learner, auxiliary credit can choose a bundle whose future purchase fullY query-macro NDCG20 exceeds the purchase-only selector's bundle. The relevant result is this downstream contrast; auxiliary BCE, gradient alignment or different selected IDs cannot satisfy it. No gain is asserted.

**Nearest source-free falsifier / nearest existing experiment.** The existing HYP065 comparison changes the shared encoder with an auxiliary loss. Here, changing invented view/cart labels must change neither purchase updates nor purchase-only credits. A two-feature analytic fixture below tests the credit and detachment contract; a three-logit fixture rejects the stronger claim that alignment protects ranking. These cannot establish useful feature selection. The nearest eventual quality falsifier is one locked bundle per selector, evaluated through identical fresh purchase-only B41 fits on one newly reserved panel.

**Task1 benefit.** Dense past mature behavioral outcomes may help allocate a small program budget without replacing the purchase objective. The potential benefit is better automatically chosen strict-past features for B41. Auxiliary data does not create missing purchase positives, restore exposure, or recover purchase targets outside fixed C.

## Concrete adaptation and temporal contract

Freeze at most eight already legal, scalar, bounded-cost programs before reading their outcomes. No proposal generation loop, nested tuning or program replacement is included. Each output retains the existing AST semantics; gates only measure attribution. Fit transforms on the predetermined mature warmup prefix, then freeze them. Optional inputs are `m_j*x_j` with `m=1`; gates are not trained parameters. A 49-input, 32-hidden-unit ReLU proxy with three scalar heads has1699 parameters. No IDs/sequence encoder, dropout, sparse-topology search or teacher is introduced.

Use query-macro binary horizon losses on the same mature Q×C: purchase P, observed view V and cart K in `[t,t+24h)`. Independent view/cart labels need not contain purchase; no exposure or verified funnel is assumed. Retain all mature all-zero queries and binary duplicate collapse. Do not insert off-C positives. Empty-C queries remain in final evaluation. Common maturity uses the maximum declared delay across P/V/K; censored labels are unknown, never fabricated zeros.

Shared parameters and the purchase head receive only `L_P`. Auxiliary head parameters receive their own BCE using `stop_gradient(h)`. At checkpoints, a separately charged differentiation pass through fixed h computes gate vectors `g_P,g_V,g_K`; it updates no parameters. For nonzero vectors, define

`c_Pj = |g_Pj| / ||g_P||_1`

`c_Aj = c_Pj + (0.1/2) * sum_(e=V,K) max(0,cos(g_P,g_e)) * |g_ej|/||g_e||_1`.

Zero auxiliary norm contributes zero; zero purchase norm skips that checkpoint. No nonzero purchase checkpoint means STOP, not auxiliary-only selection. Average eight checkpoint credits; select three programs with a frozen AST-key tie rule. This is scalar credit for one whole bundle, not a claim to measure conditional contribution or feature interactions. Correlated programs can consume all three slots. If both bundles coincide, record a null selector effect; do not search for a different weight or pool.

Choose one warmup prefix by time before labels, capped at2000Q/440000 candidate rows, with no group truncation. Proposed readiness requires at least32 purchase-positive queries from16 users; these engineering thresholds are unvalidated. Count actual distinct support only under future authority. Failure stops the arm without moving its prefix until support appears. The supplied619 purchase targets in the full training context do not establish these thresholds or independent support. All-negative batches still produce purchase gradients, so nonzero cosine does not certify positive-purchase evidence.

Both selectors become available only after the shared proxy, transforms and credit/bundle decisions are complete. Every downstream training anchor must be later than that availability. Do not backfill selected programs into historical anchors and call them prospective. Program features still use only globally strict-past events at each later anchor; auxiliary outcomes are training labels only. If common later training and fresh selection support do not exist, defer rather than recycle opened development or closed heldouts.

## Executed tiny falsifiers: what alignment does not establish

Own stdlib arithmetic, no autodiff or fitted model: two candidate rows have program inputs `((1,0),(0,1))`, purchase labels `(1,0)`, view labels `(1,1)`. A linear purchase head has weights `(1.02,.98)`, bias−1; a view head `(0,−1)`, bias0. Gate gradients are `gP=(-.50490017,.48510016)` and `gV=(0,.73105858)`. Cart gradient is zero. This is not proof that the second feature is inferior after boosting: the two literal columns are complements. It explicitly checks the selector's behavior, not downstream utility.

A separate generic update counterexample uses logits `(.01,0,10)`, relevance `(1,0,0)` and a constructed auxiliary gradient `(3,−3,5)`. Its cosine with purchase BCE gradient is+.25015031. Step `z' = z−.1*(gP+cos*gAux)` lowers summed purchase BCE11.38135226→11.18155834 while demoting the positive from rank2 to3: NDCG20 .63092975→.5. The purchase-only step keeps rank2. The auxiliary vector is a constructed differentiable direction, not a claimed trained view/cart gradient. This refutes a generic alignment-to-ranking implication; our proposed method does not apply that auxiliary update to the trunk.
