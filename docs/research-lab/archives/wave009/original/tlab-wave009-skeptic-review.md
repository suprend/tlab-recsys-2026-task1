## Primary claims: correct boundaries, restricted code evidence

AutAuT is a **NeurIPS2025 molecular auxiliary-task method**, with LLM-assisted task retrieval and gradient-alignment/Fisher-based adaptation. Its procedure includes validation feedback; it is not a free fixed-credit rule. The relevant weighting discussion extends into §3.4, so the draft's §§3.2–3.3 locator is incomplete. In the separately inspected `main_GNN.py` and `gnn_runner.py` paths, scratch/train/pretrain route to the same ordinary runner, with train/pretrain marked TBD; the runner evaluates train/validation/test and obtains predictions each epoch, then selects by validation. This supports a limited implementation caveat, **not repository-wide absence** or a reproduced paper. Repeated validation and prediction passes belong in any future reproduction's ledger. Exact inspected blob pins are in the skeptic ledger; mutable `main` and metadata modification dates are not releases. [Primary paper](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/61c2975281d60d3b1ce4cefc157d99df-Paper-Conference.pdf), [entrypoint](https://github.com/zhiqiangzhongddu/AUTAUT/blob/main/code/main_GNN.py), [runner](https://github.com/zhiqiangzhongddu/AUTAUT/blob/main/code/gnns/gnn_runner.py).

GradEnFS is **AISTATS2024**. The inspected code computes batch-mean absolute input gradients and accumulated importance, then top-k sparsity. It supports a learned credit pattern, not auxiliary-label transfer or recommendation quality. MB's absolute aggregate gate gradient is a different statistic: gradients −0.5,+0.5 have absolute aggregate zero but mean absolute value 0.5. Neither statistic alone measures conditional gain after boosting. [Primary paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v238/liu24f.html), [training](https://github.com/KaitingLiu/GradEnFS/blob/main/main.py), [importance](https://github.com/KaitingLiu/GradEnFS/blob/main/gradenfs.py).

Du et al.'s older foundation concerns cosine-weighted auxiliary parameter updates and convergence of a differentiable main loss under assumptions. It gives no finite-step NDCG or feature-selection guarantee; the paper also discusses noisy alignment and slower convergence. MB correctly distinguishes its credit-only adaptation. ESMM's impression/click/conversion domain and factorization do not turn fixed C into impressions or independently observed view/cart into a verified funnel. No CTR/CVR or causal preference conclusion transfers. [Du et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/1812.02224v2), [ESMM](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1804.07931v2).

## Reproduced arithmetic and its limits

The separate [literal results](/tmp/tlab-wave009-literal-results.json) reproduce both authors' important witnesses with own stdlib arithmetic, without autodiff or trained heads.

* MB's two-row construction has positive cosine **0.6928260942** and purchase credits **(0.51,0.49)**, yet auxiliary credits **(0.51,0.5246413047)** reverse top-one choice. This verifies routing can override purchase credit. Complementary columns prevent interpreting the chosen feature as proven inferior after final learning.
* MB's constructed auxiliary direction lowers purchase BCE **11.3813522600→11.1815583429** while NDCG@20 falls **0.6309297536→0.5**; the purchase-only step preserves rank. This refutes a general alignment-to-ranking implication. The constructed direction is not a trained view/cart gradient, and MB does not apply that auxiliary update to its trunk.
* RA's earlier purchase scores tie at **0.5**, while auxiliary scores prefer F **1 versus 0**. Two indistinguishable earlier worlds produce opposite later winners. The hand-defined W gives F−W **−0.5** or **+0.3690702464**. These are literal rankings, not tuned-W scores or an augmentation-fit result.
* RA's nested union-any example yields **0.9032867982 versus 0.1532867982**, while purchase prefers the opposite ordering. Inclusion of purchase in the auxiliary set therefore does not guarantee ranking agreement.

No literal establishes detachment implementation, source throughput, generality, rare-user support or recommendation benefit. The eight-query construction can falsify a guarantee; it cannot estimate an effect distribution.

## Contract gaps that matter before any real pilot

**1. Finite input encoding and physical closure.** Existing boosting preserves numeric NULL as NaN and has no fitted imputer. A 49-input ReLU proxy needs an explicit finite encoding before its loss or gradients are defined. MB's 1,699 parameters are correct only for exactly 49 finite inputs: `(49+1)×32+(32+1)×3`. Freeze each input's value/missing/support closure, prefix-fitted transforms and available time. Three scalar program identities do not necessarily mean three physical columns, and eight optional ASTs do not necessarily mean eight columns. Do not silently drop missing/support flags or assume the current B41+three consumer accepts a different closure. Both final arms require the same declared capacity and closure policy. This is an interface gap, not an assertion that every actual row contains NaN. [Accepted interface](</Users/mdpospelov/tlab-recsys-2026/docs/experiments/boosting-baselines.md:11>), [current three-column consumer](</Users/mdpospelov/tlab-recsys-2026/scripts/program_model_data.py:70>).

**2. Training detachment versus attribution.** MB already calls for a separate differentiation pass. Its executable contract must distinguish detached training readouts from checkpoint attribution: recompute differentiable `h(x*m)` with fixed weights, then differentiate losses with respect to gates. Differentiating a cached detached hidden tensor cannot produce the intended auxiliary input-gate gradient. A future literal implementation gate must show that changing auxiliary labels leaves trunk/purchase parameters and purchase-only credits identical under fixed batches, with isolated optimizer/RNG state, normalization, schedules and clipping. This review did not execute that implementation. The shared proxy may isolate credit use only after this invariant is demonstrated.

**3. Positive support is not fullY support alone.** MB's proposed 32 purchase-positive queries/16 users must specify retrieved-positive training support. FullY positives can all be outside C, leaving no positive proxy rows; a nonzero all-negative BCE gradient does not repair that. Report fullY-positive Q, retrieved-positive Q/rows/users and informative groups separately. The accepted whole-train counts of 619 fullY targets and 444 retrieved targets cannot certify the earlier warmup. Do not move the prefix, filter to buyers or refill after failure. [Accepted aggregate table](</Users/mdpospelov/tlab-recsys-2026/docs/backlog-reviews/tlab017-collection-verification.md:5>).

**4. Compare with the task1 incumbent.** MB's B41 baseline plus two bundle fits isolates selector routing, but beating B41 does not show improvement over accepted W=B41+R2. FB07 already states this boundary. Retain either a compatible W reference with paid scoring/authentication, or an explicitly frozen equal-capacity replacement design; a necessary new W fit increases the reservation. RA explicitly requires W and rejects an unsupported two-fit envelope if a new baseline is needed. No current016 slot or old reserved panel may supply this missing comparison. [Existing comparator boundary](</Users/mdpospelov/tlab-recsys-2026/docs/research-lab/brainstorm-fb002.md:47>).

**5. Matched purchase-only control is substantive.** MB's shared three-head proxy charges auxiliary training even for the purchase-credit bundle; report the cheaper standalone purchase-only logical work separately. RA correctly gives P two additional purchase probes rather than wasting them. Its six unique responses implement eight policy opportunity credits because two purchase responses are shared. This contrasts allocation of feedback, with different visited ASTs; it does not isolate auxiliary loss. Equal credits alone do not equal physical CPU, source reads or positive support. Failures, undefined responses, duplicate probes and all-zero cohorts consume opportunities; no post-outcome refill or label-definition switch is allowed.

The small MB payload error is editorial; the closure and checkpoint-cohort ambiguities are material. MB's six CPU-hour/four wall-hour envelope and RA's source/quality ceilings are unmeasured, ungranted proposals. Preparation includes labels, feature histories, source scans/cache authentication, transforms, both policies, failed searches, metrics and cleanup. A small label/gradient payload does not bound processing of history. Model scoring, repeated paper validation and auxiliary heads are not free because they share a cache. Parent main015 actual-cost/proof work retains priority.

## Chronology and narrow next decision

Both drafts correctly require independently defined binary horizon outcomes on the same fixed Q×C, common maximum maturity/availability delay, all mature zero-purchase queries, full purchase Y including off-C items and strict global `<anchor` history. Dense positives becoming visible early do not mature negative labels. Independent view/cart need not contain purchase; union-any does, but changes the definition and does not remove mismatch. No source-cost/quality claim is inferred from those mathematical definitions.

Proxy fitting, transform fitting, feedback, selected closure and final learner must all finish before their declared downstream use. MB's stricter requirement that downstream training anchors follow selector availability must survive the eventual calendar; old anchors cannot be retroactively described as served by the later selector. RA's fitted final rankers must likewise be available before evaluation. Adaptive bundle/label choices consume feedback and make selected uncertainty descriptive; confirmation requires a separate untouched reservation.

The first useful future falsification is a **bounded literal controller/encoding/detachment check**, attached to existing records, not a new mandatory framework: preserve the author's fixed pool and stopping rules; demonstrate complete closure, parameter invariance, nonzero recomputed attribution, undefined/no-tie/duplicate consumption and label endpoint/maturity behavior. Only after a separately authorized source gate can the fixed matched P-versus-auxiliary comparison and W reference be considered. No execution admission, positive generality claim or current pipeline change follows from this review.
