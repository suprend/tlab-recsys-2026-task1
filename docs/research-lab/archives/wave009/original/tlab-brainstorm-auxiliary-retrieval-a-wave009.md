## Label definitions and one useful primary-source boundary

For an earlier anchor s and item i, define `yP=1{purchase(i) in[s,s+24h)}` and `yVC=1{view(i) OR cart(i) in[s,s+24h)}`. All labels are **past relative to the later selector use**, not known at s. A zero means no recorded event of that definition within a complete observed horizon; it is not rejection or an unshown impression. No `yP ≤ yVC` funnel is guaranteed. If the auxiliary target is instead union-any including purchase, inclusion follows by construction, but its definition and credits change explicitly. Binary duplicate collapse is lawful for these labels; input history continues to preserve raw rows.

[ESMM's primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1804.07931v2), §§2.1–2.3, works on an impression domain with sequential click/conversion labels and shared representation. Its CTR/joint-conversion factorization is not a license to treat REES46 C as impressions or view/cart as a verified purchase funnel. This source boundary is already recognized in the frozen HYP065 note; no architecture or empirical gain transfers here. No author implementation was run.

Both selectors receive the same strictly past view/cart/purchase **input features**. The purchase-only control must not lose view/cart features merely because it does not receive auxiliary outcome supervision. Both final W+AST rankers use the same purchase objective, population, weights, parameters, training duration and scoring prefix. Changing inputs, neural heads, loss and search simultaneously would not isolate auxiliary feature selection.

## A literal counterexample that passes a purchase-tie guard

Use two periods, four mature queries per period, C containing A,B and nineteen other products, all already observable before each anchor. Two fixed hypothetical AST orderings F/G swap A and B between ranks 1/21; the middle products retain identical ranks. This is a declared score/order fixture, not a fitted model or real prediction.

In the earlier period, each query records view/cart on A. Only one query has a purchase, on a middle product at rank 3 under both F and G. Thus their purchase-proxy NDCG@20 is exactly .5, while view/cart-proxy NDCG is 1 for F and 0 for G. The guarded auxiliary rule chooses F even though it never accepts an observed purchase-proxy decrement. Dense auxiliary positives span four queries; there is still only one observed purchase-positive user if that buyer is unique.

Two later worlds preserve that view/cart signal. In world H the only purchase target is B; in world G it is A. F/G later conditional purchase NDCG is (0,1) in world H and (1,0) in world G. Keep one literal incumbent W fixed across both worlds: A is rank2 and B is rank3. Its scores are respectively .5 and `1/log2(3)≈.630930`, so auxiliary choice F loses .5 in world H or gains about .369070 in world G. That W is only a hand-defined reference, not a claim of measured tuned-model quality. A real augmentation ranker need not reproduce the direct AST ordering; proving its effect requires the later controlled fit test.

The earlier purchase proxy and auxiliary observations cannot distinguish these future worlds. Neither a denser target nor a purchase-tie safeguard guarantees positive transfer. A purchase-only seeded tie rule could choose G; that would win world H and lose world G. The fixture demonstrates uncertainty/alternative explanations, not universal superiority of either policy.

Even nested union-any does not force proxy/purchase ranking agreement. On four queries with auxiliary A everywhere and purchase B only once, union-any contains `{A,B}` on that one query and `{A}` elsewhere. F's union-any NDCG average is `(3+1/D2)/4≈.903287`; G's is `(1/D2)/4≈.153287`, where `D2=1+1/log2(3)`. Yet conditional purchase NDCG remains F=0,G=1. This extra arithmetic uses the same literal ranks and shows that label inclusion alone does not solve the target mismatch.

## The cheapest matched search control

Freeze a legal bank  ≤ 8 ASTs and a **fit-free** scoring functional: directly order each fixed C by AST value, fixed missing/tie convention, and compute the declared full-horizon conditional NDCG@20 for the specified label definition. This is an intentionally weak, cheap search proxy for the final W+AST ranker, not its measured conditional utility. Empty-positive label cohorts return undefined, not 0 or an auxiliary fallback disguised as purchase evidence. The scalar's target-dependent access is charged. No threshold learning, encoder or surrogate fit is included.

Both policies first receive purchase scores of the same two ASTs a,b. Purchase-only policy P spends two remaining credits on purchase scores for two further frozen-bank ASTs c,d, then chooses by the purchase score/fixed tie rule. Auxiliary policy V spends its two remaining credits on VC scores for a,b **only if** their purchase scores tie under the preregistered comparator, then uses VC as tie-break. Otherwise it keeps the purchase winner and stops, with no replacement use of the unused allowance. Both policies have four maximum label-feedback credits and one final selected closure. The common bank is identical; visited tuples and candidate counts may differ because allocation is the intervention.

A label credit is one `(AST ID,label-definition,cohort/horizon)` utility response. Two shared P responses plus P(c),P(d),VC(a),VC(b) yield at most 6 unique computed responses and 8 policy opportunity credits. Separate view and cart scores would cost two responses per AST; their fixed OR target costs one declared response. Repeating a deterministic purchase score is not an equivalent source of new information. Every zero/undefined response, duplicate, invalid AST and failed attempt consumes its allocated opportunity without refill. A no-tie auxiliary phase may save actual work; this is reported separately from its reserved credits.

Comparing V with purchase-only over just a,b would answer conditional auxiliary value **given extra feedback**, not overall search efficiency. Forcing that control to waste its spare credits cannot establish resource superiority. Conversely, P's extra candidate access means this allocation contrast does not isolate a pure auxiliary-loss effect. State the claim before outcomes. One additional α=0/unused-auxiliary-path arm would be a different paid comparison, not a free diagnostic or architecture result.
