## What is new information, and what is not?

Let Dv24,Dc168,Dp24 denote R2. B already has raw item views Nv24 and purchases Np24, distinct viewers Dv168, purchase popularity Dp168 from retrieval, and user/pair history. Therefore Dv24≤min(Nv24,Dv168) and Dp24≤min(Np24,Dp168), but neither shorter-window distinct is determined by those bounds. Dc168 adds a global cart-channel statistic absent from manual18; the existing pair/user cart counts do not determine the item total.

The candidate pool takes top200 by Dp168 and recent20, deduplicates without refill, and then attaches **full** Dp168 even for recent-only candidates. Thus Dp168 is an exact pre-existing alias, while Dp24 is not. Top200 membership does not create another feature novelty. On fixed C, R2 cannot recover an off-C target or improve candidate coverage; any gain is a permutation/learner effect inside the already chosen pool. Its value may depend on that pool and eligibility population, so it cannot establish portability to a different retriever.

At fixed t/item, these three counts are the same for every query user. They retain how many distinct IDs acted, not who those users were or how their profiles match u. A user-dependent effect can arise through interaction with B's history, but L05 alone is not the pre-action audience-profile mechanism of note05. Self-inclusion is legal under the declared source contract, not automatically leakage.

## A tempting control that does not remove conditional information

For active query user u, exact subtraction gives `Dminus=D−Iu`, one indicator per channel/window. In B, Iv24 is determined by pair-view recency in `(0,86400]` seconds; Ic168 by pair-cart7d>0; Ip24 by pair-purchase recency in `(0,86400]`. The upper age endpoint is inclusive because the window is `[t−24h,t)`; all peers at t are excluded. A known absent history gives indicator0; unknown source coverage must remain a distinct failure/coverage state.

Consequently `(B,D)` and `(B,Dminus)` recover each other: `D=Dminus+I(B)`. Removing u does not remove information conditional on the full B. A fixed-capacity boosting model may behave differently after the transformation, but this is a representation test, not an ablation proving the gain came from self-history or other-user compatibility. The same warning applies to residualizing D against a deterministic function of B while preserving that function. No learned propensity model or uncharged residual fit is necessary for this algebraic point.

## Minimal distinguishing future contrast, with an honest stronger control

Simply replacing R2 with raw counts is inadequate: Nv24 and Np24 already exist in B, so a purported B+3 raw block would contain two aliases. Duplicated inputs can change finite-learner behavior but are not three new pieces of information. Do not silently treat such a degenerate arm as equally informative.

Define a common stronger feature set **S=B+Nc168**, with raw global item cart count added once. Compare **A=S+(Dv24,Dc168,Dp24)** against **M=S+(log1p Nv24,log1p Nc168,log1p Np24)**. M has the same raw activity observables as S, plus three deterministic numeric representations; A supplies breadth beyond those observables. Both retain the full tuned manual B, same query/user history and retrieval context. With ordinary unconditional count semantics, S has42 inputs and both arms45; their union adds7 physical columns relative to B. A also contains current W plus one raw-cart count. No current registry or model is edited.

Log1p is an illustrative **control transformation**, not a new mechanism. It needs reviewed nonnegative-count/type/float semantics and compiler/oracle support before any actual admission. Known zero maps to0; missing source state does not become observed0. Exact dependencies and shared coverage are reused only by semantic identity.
