## Refinements required in the interpretation

**Tree count is not a complete work count.** RA's 202 is correctly labeled syntactic trees, not distinct functions. It can be the number of unique final-tree validations. It is not automatically every generation attempt: straightforward staged enumeration performs `2+2×2²+2×10² = 210` bindings, including lower-depth duplicates. A memoized enumerator may account differently; record its real construction/duplicate/canonicalization work. This is a cost-unit qualification, not an error in the recurrence or a demand for more main testing.

**Exact rational identity must stay inside the toy.** RA explicitly excludes floating-point rewrites; preserve that exclusion in publication and any future adapter. Even bounded nonnegative floating expressions can lose associativity. An own binary64 witness, followed by final float32 encoding, gives different outputs for `(x+y)+y` and `x+(y+y)` at invented x=1−3·2⁻²⁵,y=2⁻⁵⁴. These are not actual category-share observations. The witness illustrates why a rational-polynomial proof cannot authorize merging future production trees with different evaluation order. No current Add/Mul implementation is claimed or admitted.

**Full closure governs eligibility.** MB's mock supports are not actual B41. RA's two category shares do exist in manual18; their historical category counts, current-category resolution, denominators and missingness are dependencies. Both selected outputs depend on the same x/y NULL union. Two values plus a shared new union flag may fit three physical columns, but the exact flag must be compared with the full W registry, not inferred from a similar category flag or from root count. Required existing supports/coverage must remain available and costs charged. Current016 consumes B41 plus exactly three physical additions; a future W augmentation is a separate consumer contract. [Registry](</Users/mdpospelov/tlab-recsys-2026/configs/features/manual-18.json>), [emission](</Users/mdpospelov/tlab-recsys-2026/scripts/feature_programs.py:48>).

**Unlabeled diversity is an inductive bias.** Rank-sketch distance is neither semantic distance nor conditional utility given W. A fixed 32-coordinate sketch can miss rare informative differences; a rare-support gate can remove the future winner. Canonical representative root strata are bookkeeping choices, not independent evidence of complementary information. RA correctly requires shared applicability eligibility and actual closure. Its distance formula, NULL weighting, rank/tie normalization and thresholds are still future contract items; one predeclared version must replace “large distance” before implementation. No purchase-guided choice among those options may remain labeled outcome-free.

**Separate resource claims from matched-information claims.** Common applicability computation may genuinely be necessary for both arms. Charge its shared physical work once and each policy's use honestly. Distance-only sketch/pair-search work must also be reported for the selector; the control should not be forced to waste work merely to make standalone costs equal. A cheaper seeded grammar without the common data-dependent eligibility rule would be a different comparison, not an implicitly added arm. Shared source artifacts do not establish end-to-end efficiency against that simpler protocol.
