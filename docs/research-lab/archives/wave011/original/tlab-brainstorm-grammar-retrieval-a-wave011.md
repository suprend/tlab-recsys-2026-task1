## Cheapest literal falsifiers

**Unlabeled diversity does not identify purchase utility.** Invent 16 queries with identical two-item C={a,b}. Baseline W orders b before a on every query. Two legal hypothetical scalar outputs have x(a)=1 only at q1 and y(a)=1 only at q2; all other x/y values, including b, are zero. Ranking by either scalar uses W to break ties. Warmup/source features and all policy information are identical in two possible worlds. World X has a single positive query q1 buying a; world Y instead has a single positive query q2 buying a. Other queries have no purchase. Selecting x gives conditional-positive-query NDCG@20 equal to 1 in X and `1/log2(3)=0.63092975` in Y; selecting y reverses the result. W is 0.63092975 in either world. These are prescribed literal ranking functions, not fitted models or observed outcomes. The label-free policy must make the same choice in both worlds. This disproves a universal implication from source diversity to utility; it does not disprove an empirical inductive bias. One positive among16 is kept in the correct conditional denominator, never divided by16 to manufacture small headroom.

**Empirical aliasing can remove a future useful branch.** Warmup assignments with x=y make x+y and 2x equal, but x=0,y=1 separates them. Conversely x+y and y+x are universally equal in strict ordinary addition. An observed equality cache must not declare the first pair a semantic alias; exact commutative canonicalization may identify the second. Raw syntax diversity may therefore count duplicates, while output diversity may suppress distinct programs. Neither is a semantic proof. A rare-support gate is a resource choice that may discard the only predictive signal.

**Invariants cannot supply the missing utility signal.** Constant zero passes future-suffix and peer-permutation invariance. A wrong doubled raw count can pass additivity and bag-scaling laws. Before evaluating proposal diversity, require literal goldens for raw duplicates, lower/upper window boundaries, all equal-time peers and NULL propagation against an independently specified list oracle. Passing these laws admits a program's meaning; it does not earn purchase credit. These failure modes are already round045, not another selector family.
