**Disposition:** REFINE metrics006/protocol013/014/015/019 and rounds019/069; NEW0/ADMIT0. Waves071/072 already preserve prospective-calendar and selected-procedure limits. No new mandatory test, CV, or resampling gate.

**Mechanism.** User resampling can describe a fixed B/W contrast conditional on the realized six-day calendar, eligibility, item/source context and fitted pipelines, when user clusters are plausibly conditionally independent/exchangeable and the sparse ratio has adequate support. “Fixed dates” alone does not prove those assumptions: residual item/social dependence can remain. Conditional-on-positive-fullY is a separate estimand restriction from conditional-on-calendar. [Metrics006](/Users/mdpospelov/tlab-recsys-2026/scripts/ranking_metrics.py:203) retains all mature user clusters and one shared multiplicity for B/W; it neither resamples calendar contexts nor retrains.

**Counterhypothesis.** A shared shock affects both models equally and cancels in their difference, or conditioning removes the dependence. Shared context therefore does not automatically invalidate conditional user inference. Differential model exposure to a common shock, however, can survive pairing. [Cameron–Miller](https://cameron.econ.ucdavis.edu/research/Cameron_Miller_JHR_2015_February.pdf), abstract/§II.F, supports the between-cluster independence requirement; its regression results do not certify coverage for our sparse ranking ratio.

**Finite MANUAL prediction, UNRUN.** Stipulate two users, six daily queries each, identical C={p,q}, fullY={p}. In common calendar state S+, every B ranking is [q,p] and W ranking [p,q]. Every query difference is a=1−1/log2(3)>0; every possible user-multiplicity reweighting predicts a. In a possible future state S−, reverse both rankings: difference −a. The same frozen scoring procedures may respond differently to changed context. More users or bootstrap repetitions cannot identify the frequency of S− from S+ alone. This is a logical witness, not evidence that such shocks occurred.
