# Wave032 B FIRST — shared MinHash, explicit approximation

**Disposition:** zero new HYPs; computational refinement of HYP001/TLAB012. Jaccard is already proposed. Approximation cannot inherit HYP046's exact-equality claim; this is fixed-C scoring, without ANN/retrieval changes.

**Mechanism.** For each item, sketch its distinct view-user set in a declared seven-day snapshot. Shared hash/permutation identities permit collision-fraction Jaccard estimates; aggregate mean/max over at most20 history items, excluding the diagonal. Datasketch v1.6.5 implements coordinate-min updates/unions and collision comparison.[1] Our proposed two-minima-per-coordinate extension retains distinct user IDs to exclude query-user u from both sets; it is not stock behavior or general deletion support. Weighted MinHash estimates weighted Jaccard, not cosine; raw event multiplicity would change the weighted target.[2] Keep the binary variant first.

**Prediction.** Let A={1,…,10}, B={5,6}, C={1,2,3,4,11,…,16}. Exact Jaccard ranks C above B: .25 versus .2; cosine reverses this: .4 versus √.2. Two permutations starting (5,6,…) and (6,5,…) produce estimated Jaccard1 for B and0 for C. Their exact intersections are2 and4: collisions cannot certify support≥3. Identical singleton audiences have Jaccard1 yet become empty after removing their sole query-user. High overlap does not supply independent support.

**Nearest finite UNRUN falsifier.** Specify those sets, the singleton/empty cases, both complete16-ID permutations, and expiry of5 then6 without arrivals. Recompute exact membership and sketch minima at each state; retained two minima cannot recover A's next minimum after both expire. Scope: ≤23 initial memberships,2 permutations,46 initial rank lookups; no executed fixture or fitted model. Test masks, self-exclusion, ties and boundary peers independently. Daily rebuilding is the safe proposed contract; rolling expiry needs additional paid state/rebuilds.

**Task1 benefit/control.** Measure whether approximation reduces complete compatibility-feature cost without unacceptable distortion. Bounded exact Jaccard uses identical snapshots, history, Q/C/fullY, aggregation and NULL policy; optimized exact control builds sorted postings once and caches unique requested intersections before personal subtraction. Do not replace it with repeated uncached scans. Eligible strong B/W remain quality controls in the same future context; no new quality look now. Threshold/shrinkage support requires exact verification or an explicitly changed feature definition.

**Chronology/cost/negative outcome.** Snapshot cutoff s<t, actual bank availability≤t, whole-window coverage and age are explicit in both arms; same-time peers are excluded. For E scanned events, I items, k coordinates and R≤20Σ|Cq| requested pairs: sketch build O(kE), state O(2kI), query O(kR). Exact degrees/dedup/expiry can still require O(D) distinct-edge state. Pay all global scans, warm-up, versions, hashing, index/native work, spill, masks, sink and failures. No reservation/grant. If optimized exact is cheaper, low-support distortion dominates, or future B/W gain fails, defer.
