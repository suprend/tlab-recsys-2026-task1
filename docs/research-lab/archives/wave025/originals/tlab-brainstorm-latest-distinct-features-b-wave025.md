# Wave025 FIRST — latest occurrence suffices; the expiry representation matters

15 October2026. Blind independent FIRST; no peer025 read. **Zero novelty/IDs:** refine HYP046 and FeaturesA wave005 A005-02. Scope is only item DISTINCT-users for view24h/cart168h/purchase24h (round033 L05), not replacement of recency/category/other B41 state.

**Mechanism/proof.** For each non-NULL user, item and action, let ℓ be its maximum qualifying timestamp in the immutable observed prefix `[S,t)`. Membership in `[max(S,t−W),t)` holds iff ℓ≥max(S,t−W): a qualifying occurrence implies its maximum is inside; conversely that maximum witnesses membership. Missing ℓ contributes nothing. Duplicate copies change no membership; raw counters elsewhere retain multiplicity. Process authenticated nondecreasing event time, or pay for sorting; snapshots precede **all global peers at t**, including users outside Q. Whole-future maxima are invalid. Advance expiry at every query anchor, even without arrivals: remove only ℓ<t−W, retaining equality. Wall/arrival clocks never trigger eviction. Retractions, corrections or backward anchors invalidate this specialization.

Each action has one fixed window, so no multiwindow rank structure is necessary: dictionary `(item,action,user)→latest,node`, one timestamp-ordered removable list per action, and per-item/action membership counters. Renewal unlinks the old node and appends one replacement; same-timestamp copies can be skipped. Eviction removes a list head and decrements its counter. This is a specialization of the earlier proposal, not an invented DISTINCT algorithm.

**Source facts versus adaptation.** [Feldera's2024 author article](https://www.feldera.com/blog/time-series) conditions state elimination on truthful ordering/append-only properties and distinguishes retention from deletion. It does not prove our list implementation. [DuckDB1.5 docs](https://duckdb.org/docs/current/sql/functions/aggregates) specify DISTINCT/NULL and count semantics; they do not promise this physical plan. REES46 event_time supplies no ingestion-availability guarantee.

**Prediction/cost.** Repeated users may make one live node/key cheaper than an occurrence queue plus refcounts. The full specialized state is K dictionary entries+K list nodes+G item/action counters, encoded keys/pointers/allocator overhead included. A lazy heap with a stale notice per renewal can instead retain O(N) entries; charge stale-pop/compaction and temporary rebuild peaks. K itself can reach N, particularly during cold build or between anchor expirations. Sorting/authentication, cold prefix reads, every update, projection, coverage/support handling, failures and complete unchanged B41+3 sink remain paid. No minimal-memory or full-pipeline saving is established.
