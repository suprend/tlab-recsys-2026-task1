The proposed change is confined to pair-source key membership; the paired control is unchanged005, with the same registry and optimizer settings. Removing `P\keys(Ct)` is safe for the reviewed direct pair counts/recencies because these off-C pair groups do not feed any requested pair scalar. Take one label-blind selected query u at t; source starts at t−10d. There are20 recently viewed products i,r1…r19, each with one u-view at t−1h and known category A. Two older u-views of k/category B occur at t−2h and one second earlier. Two exact duplicate v-views of j/category A occur at t−2h. Add one v-view of i/category A at t−3h, one u-view of z/category B at t−9d and one u-purchase of i/category A at t−8d. These are27 past rows. Add two global-peer events of i exactly at t, one category A and one NULL; they are excluded altogether. Total29 invented rows, no target table.

There are no purchases in seven days, so popular source is empty. The20 more recent distinct u-items form recent20; k,j,z are outside this query's C. This avoids pretending that a second recent item could disappear from a tiny20-quota fixture.

## Lazy materialization is not free shared state

Ct is available from the accepted strict-past retrieval input before feature work at each anchor. Do not use a union of later candidate sets to claim that an earlier online planner knew its future demand. An offline batch may declare that planning boundary, but must not claim the same online availability. Exact nominal keys and whole timestamp buckets remain mandatory; candidate permutation does not alter requested-key membership.

A newly demanded pair/item may have older relevant history not present in a lazy cache. It needs complete backfill of its window and full prefix for recency. Starting state at zero, reusing a stale earlier anchor or skipping overflow changes the feature. Shared subexpressions require the same source version, cutoff/available_at, key grain, action/window/projection, metadata policy, multiplicity and NULL semantics. Different source demand is not a semantic alias. The complete dependency closure must be ready before the consuming feature output; no fitted warmup/statistical selector is proposed here, but state initialization and backfill still cost source work.

A negative logical workload is also explicit: all historical pairs for selected users are already in C. Then P equals requested groups, so this rewrite saves no aggregate group and adds a demand-table lookup. A second negative workload has sparse requested groups but a compressed source that must still be fully decoded for both variants. Logical row/state reduction may coexist with equal or higher whole-plan CPU. No index or physical page pruning is assumed from a SQL semijoin or CTE alone.
