# Wave061 B — descriptive subset stability

Mechanism: selection frequency measures sensitivity to a declared perturbation, not truth or predictive utility. Meinshausen–Bühlmann assume i.i.d. observations; their false-selection bound additionally requires exchangeable noise selections and a procedure at least as good as random guessing. Shah–Samworth CPSS drops those latter conditions for bounds about low-selection-probability variables, but retains i.i.d. units; stronger bounds add shape assumptions. Whole-user/time perturbations here do not establish those premises. Neither theorem supplies ranking improvement or an FDR guarantee for this dependent, adaptive study.

Nearest finite future experiment, **UNRUN/ungranted**: freeze the five historical B/I1/I2/R1/R2 saved-score packages, exact identities and schema-defined family map.
