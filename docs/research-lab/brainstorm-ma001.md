## MA-01 — parameter-sharing diversity can replace neural model size

**Source mechanism.** [TabM, ICLR2025; first preprint2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.24210v2) trains multiple weak MLP submodels sharing most weights, with individual adapters/heads and a mean of their losses; collective predictions improve through diversity. It is not a purchase-specific architecture. The [author repository](https://github.com/yandex-research/tabm) provides the implementation. Paper experiments commonly use32submodels and substantial tuning; neither their efficiency nor their benchmark ordering transfers automatically to CPU sparse daily ranking.

**New delta.** HYP063 tests candidate-conditioned history pooling and064 cross layers. MA01 instead tests implicit ensembling/adapter diversity on **exact B41 numeric rows**, without raw ID embeddings, new history or auxiliary heads. No item-text semantics are assumed. Hypothesis: shared weak predictors reduce instability from619purchase targets more efficiently than one larger network. Counter: submodels learn the same popularity/repeat rule, and rare positive gradients remain inadequate.

**Four fits/controls.** A fixed small MLP; TabMmini with4submodels (a deliberate adaptation from32); the same4-submodel model with tied adapter initialization; and a wider single MLP with matched parameter count. Same query-weighted BCE, scaler, minibatch query schedule, inputs and fixed training duration. Arithmetic mean of sigmoid predictions is declared before fit; no best-submodel pruning. B remains the external strong reference,0new B fits. Observables: pairwise submodel score correlation, disagreement near rank20, aggregate versus individual prediction quality, parameter/RAM/CPU/scoring cost. These explain a package result; no submodel chosen using selection labels.

**Cheapest falsification.** Source-free parameter/memory arithmetic and a literal four-head example first; then, only under a separate train-only authorization, check whether initialized adapters actually produce distinct gradients on synthetic imbalanced queries. If tied and diverse variants yield indistinguishable scores or the4-head package exceeds the cap before a useful training schedule, defer. Beating MLP but losing to B does not satisfy an alternative-method superiority claim.

## MA-02 — retrieve mature labeled rows in a learned metric, not item neighbors

**Source mechanism.** [ModernNCA,2024preprint/ICLR2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.03257) modernizes differentiable nearest-neighbor prediction with learned nonlinear representations and stochastic training neighborhoods. The [author toolkit](https://github.com/LAMDA-Tabular/TALENT) identifies it as a neighborhood-based predictor. The paper uses generic tabular classification/regression and full training neighborhoods at inference; this does not establish scalable inference over5.317mREES46 rows or prove sparse-purchase effectiveness.

**New delta.** HYP001/051 compare item/user interaction neighbors/factors;063 pools a user's history conditioned on candidate. MA02 retrieves **other mature `(q,item)` feature rows and their purchase labels**, conditioned on the entire numericB41 query-candidate state. Hypothesis: a locally similar past shopping state shares useful purchase support even when user/item IDs differ. Counter: local regions contain almost only zeros, memorize repeated users, or merely reproduce global target rate.

**Four fits/controls.** NonlinearNCA, linearNCA, capacity-matched rowwise MLP and nonlinearNCA with a once-seeded shuffled-label memory control. An unfitted normalized-distance neighbor scorer is also a paid scoring diagnostic,0fit. All use identical query-weighted binary objective/population and a predeclared deterministic support bank; memory bank rows are available mature training rows only, with self and same-user/query overlap excluded from training retrieval. Query weights must affect memory label aggregation, not just outer loss. Do not oversample positives and call the resulting posterior calibrated. Bank cap/sample law and an outside-bank fallback are fixed without outcomes; a cap changes the original method and must be reported.

**Chronology/observables.** A standalone final learner may use a bank frozen at final train cutoff for later evaluation; any rolling feature version instead needs per-anchor mature banks and additional fits, outside this candidate. Record positive mass/effective neighbor count, distinct users and age in neighborhoods, fallback fraction and inference dot/distance count. FixedC/fullY never receives a retrieved positive insertion. Cheapest authorized next step is a train-only support/cost gate, not a quality fit: if nearly all neighborhoods have zero positive mass, or bounded-bank inference is uncompetitive against B, stop. Paper-scale throughput is not a forecast.

## MA-03 — full-list top20 softmax weighting, with explicit coverage mismatch

**Source mechanism.** [SL@K, KDD2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.05673v1) introduces a quantile-based sigmoid weight on positive softmax-loss terms to emphasize the topK region. Its [author code](https://github.com/Tiny-Snow/IR-Benchmark) is linked by the paper. The theoretical development assumes nonzero DCG; an important bound distinguishes one versus multiple topK hits. Its catalog quantile/negative-sampling setting differs from our ≤220candidate list and frequent zero purchase/zero coverage. No universal NDCG guarantee is imported.

**New delta.** Existing listwise notes propose Lambda/Yeti/XE; full/sampledCE already has057/058. MA03 asks whether **exact threshold computation over every fixed candidate**, rather than a sampled catalog quantile, helps rank20, and whether training query weights acknowledge fullY coverage. It changes no retrieval. Counter: score-dependent weights suppress difficult true purchases, especially new pairs, and one observed positive offers little topK information.

**Four fits/controls.** One compact shared row scorer in all arms: query-balanced BCE; fullC softmax; SL@20 with candidate-only normalization; SL@20 with a declared fullY-derived query weight. The last is our adaptation, not an author theorem or exact differentiable NDCG. Proposed coverage weight is wq=IDCG20(|Cq∩Yq|)/IDCG20(|Yq|) when the numerator is nonzero, otherwise0; this follows the full/candidate NDCG denominator ratio, but applying it to a nonlinear loss remains an unproved adaptation. Freeze temperature and gradient-through-threshold policy from source before fit. FullY cardinality is a training label used only after maturity, never an inference feature. List-only terms are zero for no covered positive; all matured queries remain in BCE arm/evaluation. Publish their training-population difference. No all-zero list receives an invented uniform-positive target.

**Cheapest falsification.** Literal lists with0/1/2positives, partial coverage, ties and shortC should expose whether the two normalizations actually change per-query mass and whether gradients push a hard positive downward. Exact-list threshold does not mean exactNDCG optimization. On future authorized train diagnostics, quantify how much mass lies on positives currently below20; severe suppression makes this lower priority than BCE. A better candidate-only loss cannot recover off-C targets.

## MA-04 — closed-list distillation versus teacher-top20 truncation

**Source mechanism.** [RCE-KD, ICLR2026](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/a92de7efca19f26309a38b3e3fa3dd86-Abstract-Conference.html) explains that subsetCE's ranking connection requires closure around the student's top items, which a teacher-top subset can violate; it splits subsets and uses teacher-student sampling. The [author code](https://github.com/BDML-lab/RCE-KD) first trains a teacher. Their CF backbones and large item spaces differ from B41 fixedC. We can test closure directly over fullC, so importing their sampler is unnecessary.

**New delta.** No canonical HYP tests teacher/student closure or purchase ranker compression. This differs from frozen-representation hybrid and auxiliary065: teacher logits provide dense within-list supervision without another outcome/task. Hypothesis: exactfullC KL retains B's ordering better than top20-only KD at the same student capacity. Counter: it merely copies B's errors or in-sample overfit, and B300 is already cheap enough that compression has no value.

**Four fits/controls.** Same small standalone student: purchaseBCE only; BCE+fullCteacherKD; BCE+teacher-top20subsetKD; BCE+fullCKD with once-per-query shuffled teacher scores. Same teacher artifact, temperature, KD coefficient, trainQ/C/Y and training cap; these are fixed before outcomes. B replay is0fit but paid. Record teacher/student top20 overlap, student items above teacher20, teacher margin, fullYquality and measured latency/memory. No new teacher fit is hidden: if the sealedB is unusable under the new chronology, a teacher fit consumes an attempt and a control must be prospectively removed or the idea deferred.

**Availability/cheap rejection.** Teacher must exist before student training completes and before later student predictions; final standalone KD does not assert that teacher scores existed at historical training anchors. Feeding KD scores to another early-anchor booster would require a separate earlier teacher/OOF plan and paid fits. First gate is source-free closure counterexample plus explicit teacher lifecycle. Only if an authorized future benchmark identifies a real B inference bottleneck should KD merit fits. Dense soft labels do not create new independent purchases or causal exposure information.
