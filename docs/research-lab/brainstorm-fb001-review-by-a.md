## FB-01 — accept-needs-pilot with a precise order-control contract

**Primary source.** Walker et al.’s [ICML2024 paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/walker24a.html) and [§2.4 definitions](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18512v3#S2.SS4) support iterated integrals/log-signatures and the neural differential-equation context. They do not test this ecommerce scalar. The stated factor is correct: for straight-line cumulative-count increments, the antisymmetric degree-two tensor coordinate is half the printed A_ab.

**Literal check.** In VVCCVPC, each C contributes prior-V minus later-V: 1+1+3=5. In VCVCVPC it contributes −1+1+3=3. Both have V3/C3/P1 and final V5/P6/C7. The same-second (V1,C1) increment has zero antisymmetric self-area. Thus the functional nonalias is valid against counts and final recencies, including B’s pertinent primitives. This proves no utility. No fitted-state or target maturity issue arises in computing the raw statistic.

**Minimal contract.** W=[t−168h,t), exact parsed timestamp buckets, all global peers excluded before update, raw multiplicity retained, empty observed path→0. Three signed count-squared outputs, integer arithmetic before the declared model cast. Existing168h source coverage stays visible. Preserve window-edge equality and perform overflow checks before integer arithmetic. Concatenation must satisfy A(XY)=A(X)+A(Y)+N(X)∧N(Y); ordinary subtraction of expired areas is invalid.

The shuffle comparator needs a repair in wording: preserving each timestamp’s complete bucket counts preserves the path, yielding an identity control. Instead specify permutations of the **multiset of bucket vectors** among admissible times, with final action times and every raw B field fixed; restrict permutations inside B-window strata as needed. Keep B arrays immutable and record the fraction of histories with a nonidentity legal permutation. Empty permutation sets are consumed diagnostics, never redrawn until quality improves. Two downstream fits can then compare area versus shuffled-area, each also against frozen B.

**Cheapest next test.** The seven-event witness, simultaneous bucket, exact lower boundary, two-block concatenation/expiry and fixed-context future-suffix invariance are sufficient first literal gates. No source access is needed. The author’s100Q/1M-retained-record/30CPUmin/4GB and two-fit90CPUmin ceilings remain proposals. Add a physical scan plan and shared accounting before interpreting their feasibility.

**Dedup.** Existing sequence/pooling order comparisons and generic HYP002 permit related questions, but no canonical scalar area operator was found. NB-F03 pools directed category transitions; it does not preserve candidate-pair cross-action area. FA01–04 concern search policies/libraries, not this statistic.

## FB-02 — refine denominator, masks and fair support

**Primary source.** [Mayfly §§3.1–3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.07962v1#S3.SS1) supports full-window per-device contribution bounding and subsequent aggregation. cap2, HHI and maximum-share predictive features are the author’s adaptation. **Literal check and novelty.** For (5,1) versus (3,3), N6/D2 match while cap2 is3/4, HHI26/36 versus18/36, maxshare5/6 versus1/2. This is valid. Existing HHI in `docs/features/03-rates/research-b.md` measures a user’s attention over categories; reversing both entity grain and contribution distribution is substantive. Duplicate share measures equality of full rows, not an item’s viewer multiplicity. HYP021/069/070 neighbor/audience matching also differs. FA and F001 contain no identical operator.

**Material closure finding.** `configs/features/manual-18.json` supplies item_view_24h and item_distinct_viewers_7d, not item_view_7d. Generic observed-history flags cannot distinguish N7d=0 from N7d>0. R2’s item-view7d column can supply the exact denominator only if the proposed future primary control explicitly contains that frozen R2 block. The note cannot alternate B41 and W44 implicitly. Even then the native/automatic missing-mask policy must be spelled out: three numeric cores plus newly emitted masks can exceed three physical columns. Treating identical masks as reusable requires an actual declared-column identity, not an informal availability resemblance.

**Minimal repair.** Freeze the primary control and exact N7d/support/missing closure. If B41 remains primary, isolate shape versus the same B+N7d/support control; report that matched-support comparison together with the comparison against B. Either reduce cores to honor three physical additions or defer the quality bundle; this review grants no larger allocation. Keep unknown support, observed zero and incomplete168h coverage separate. Preserve raw multiplicity and global peer exclusion. Specify self-included audience; excluding the query user would be a different, more expensive candidate-dependent feature.

**Cheapest test.** The two printed histograms plus insertion and expiry should give N→N−1, Q2→Q2−2n+1 for removal from a viewer currently contributing n. Verify maximum when the unique maximum expires, cap crossing2→1, zero support and simultaneous updates. Source-free arithmetic can reject a broken state machine. Count total source rows examined separately from matched events and active(item,viewer) keys; a1M retained-key cap does not bound the raw scan or peak allocator memory.

## FB-03 — refine the observed episode semantics

**Primary source.** [POWL preprint §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.15346v1) supports partial-order event handling and loop-aware process discovery. Its v1 explicitly disclaims peer review. It supplies no shopping reset assumption or ecommerce improvement; the author correctly presents those as conjectures.

**Literal check and novelty.** With P4 and carts(1,2,7)/(1,6,7), full cart count and last cart agree; strict-after-P carts are1/2. Fixed counts, recencies, NB-F01’s pre-purchase contextual recurrence and NB-F02’s fitted residual waiting time do not recover this reset count. FB-01 retains all cross-action order, so it is not the same feature. No new source fields or order IDs are required.

**Minimal definition needed.** Set p=max observed purchase timestamp<t. Views/carts count events satisfying t−168h≤s<t and s>p. No observed p→NULL, even if some views/carts exist; do not drop those Q. p before the window→all within-window actions count. Multiple purchases at p reset once at that timestamp, without inventing order or quantity. B’s full-prefix pair_purchase_recency_seconds_missing can share the no-observed-reset mask only after exact semantics/transport verification.

The third core is currently ambiguous. Recommend carts_at_p restricted to p≥t−168h, zero if a known p is older, NULL if no observed p. Alternatively counting the older purchase peer bucket is defensible, but it changes the declared7d feature scope and requires that bucket’s raw multiplicity from the prefix. Freeze one choice before implementation. October left truncation means “no observed reset,” never “never purchased”; preserve coverage and observed age without estimating lifetime absence.

**Cheapest test.** Author witness, cart/purchase peers, p just below/equal/above the lower window boundary, no observed p, and future-suffix invariance. Confirm all three feature missing states match the intended shared mask. The last purchase comes from a prefix even when actions are7d; a bounded recent-action queue alone cannot produce it. Reuse an authenticated exact past purchase state or charge its full prefix lookup/scan. The30CPUmin/1M matched-event ceiling does not already cover that unseen work. A future matched fixed-window arm must retain the same applicability/support mask so a reset effect is not an availability effect.

## FB-04 — defer until reward and transfer feasibility are concrete

**Primary source.** [Pedersen/Crandall, ICMLA2025 §§III–VI](https://faculty.cs.byu.edu/~crandall/papers/Pedersen_ICMLA1_2025.pdf) does train target-domain per-generator KNN predictors with k15; transfer of an attention representation does not remove this learning. The paper reports a cooperative-strategy weakness. Our three raw descriptors and static arm routing reproduce neither its learned representation nor its interactive bandit environment.

**Novelty.** Query-conditional choice among trained arms differs from the current global winner, HYP014’s unavailable pretrained feature meta-ranker, NB-F04’s label-free one-time allocation, and FA03’s shared feature-program search. Keep the claim at context-conditional inference; holding out one support region is an extrapolation test, not established domain transfer.

**Required minimal contract.** Name the exact≤5 arm manifests and common Q/C/fullY. Define the third descriptor using existing7d pair counts if it is to be scan-free: fraction of C with pair_view7d+pair_cart7d+pair_purchase7d>0. “Any prior pair history” across the lifetime is unavailable from those counts and would need additional source work. Freeze past-fit scaling/distance/ties for age(seconds), breadth(counts), and fraction; unscaled Euclidean kNN mostly measures age.

Define query reward and zero-Y treatment. Conditional NDCG is undefined when Y is empty; do not silently turn that undefined value into a measured zero or discard those queries from inference. Either fit only on eligible positive mature rewards under an explicit conditional-utility estimand, or use a declared all-query reward with its matching evaluation denominator. Report informative user/query support per arm and held-out support region, not just the≤2,000 total-query cap. Freeze support thresholds and global-arm fallback when k15 is unsupported.

Forward folds must first train arms, then predict router-training queries with those already available arms, mature their rewards, fit router/scaler, and only then evaluate the router on later queries. User grouping alone does not establish this chronology. Specify the support-region boundaries before outcome inspection and keep their held-out labels out of tuning. Reusing existing development predictions is screening-only; current reserved targets remain CLOSED.

**Cheapest gate.** First a source-free fake-arm case with identical rankings must give zero router advantage, plus opposite-context rewards verifying whole-query choice and tie/fallback policy. Only later, if separately authorized, authenticate compatible development predictions and test empirical arm disagreement/support. The oracle per-query-best-arm envelope is a diagnostic label look that must be charged and cannot become an achievable performance claim. Four comparator policies and repeated fold scores are additional looks despite zero new ranker fits. The30CPUmin/4GB storage/work ceiling does not guarantee all-arm scoring fits it; all new ranking, hashing, loading, reward calculation and failed attempts belong to the same future ledger.
