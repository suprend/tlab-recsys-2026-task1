Already tried, all of it in the model above:
- a DFS grid (tables dfs_p, dfs_c, dfs_b, dfs_u): for the candidate product, its category, its brand and the
  user; windows of 1, 3, 7 and 14 days before t; for every event type log(1 + events) and log(1 + distinct
  users), plus smoothed cart-per-view and purchase-per-view rates of distinct users;
- SQL features of an earlier blind agent on an earlier period of the same store, by table (name: idea):
  r1p1_first_touch_conversion: This mirrors the target definition at the item level. Column one is the smoothed lift of P(cart or purchase within 24h | user's first touch of the item in the last 7 days). Column two is the share of the item's recent acquirers who first touched it no more than 24h before acquiring, i.e. how often the item is bought by people who were new to it.
  r1p2_coacq_cf: This is item-item similarity from co-acquisition rather than co-view: a cosine over users who carted or bought both items in the last 14 days. It is applied to the user's 10 latest touched items, so it surfaces complements and accessories that the co-view features miss.
  r1p3_buyer_price_profile: This describes the price taste of the item's buyers: the mean and spread of their median viewed log price, and the gap between that and the item's own log price. The model can match it against u_log_price_med_view_7d to tell which price-tier audiences the item converts.
  r1p4_new_reach: This measures the item's pull on new audiences, which is mostly view-based and therefore immune to the cart logging shift. It gives the share of the item's last-24h users who touched it for the first time in 14 days, plus the trend and log volume of those first-time users against the item's 7-day daily average.
  r1p5_cp_staleness: This is a stock-out or demand-stop detector: the log hours since the item's last cart or purchase, and that time divided by the item's expected inter-acquisition gap from its 7-day acquirer count. Normally popular items that suddenly go quiet score high.
  r2p1_cmp_session_win_lift: Choice share under comparison: in 14d sessions where a user viewed at least 2 items of one category and carted or bought something there, this is how often this item was the chosen one versus a uniform-choice expectation, plus the log number of such comparison sessions; it is a normalised ratio, so the cart level shift largely cancels.
  r2p2_cp_category_complement: Category-level complement graph from acquisitions: lift of P(first cart/purchase in category c2 within 3 days after one in c1) over 14 days, applied from the categories the user acquired in the last 7 days to the top 40 acquired products of each complementary category; this captures accessory demand such as phone to case that view-based links miss.
  r2p3_acq_seed_coview_cf: Session co-view neighbours (cosine, 7d, sessions of 2-30 items) of the user's 5 latest carted or bought items rather than latest views, with recency decay (72h) on the seed acquisition; this surfaces what people browse alongside things the user actually committed to, even when those items left the 20-latest-views window.
  r2p4_revisit_consideration: Consideration depth from views only, so it is immune to the cart shift: the smoothed lift over the category of the share of the item's 7d viewers who came back to it in 2 or more distinct sessions, plus the log of views per viewer.
  r2p5_view_dwell_time: Page dwell: the smoothed mean log seconds (capped at 600) between a view of the item and the next event in the same session over 7 days, and its gap to the category mean; long dwell signals serious evaluation and comes from views, so it is stable across the cart logging shift.
  r3p1_view_session_last_choice: View-only decision signal immune to the cart shift: in 7d sessions where a user viewed 3 or more items of one category, this is how often the item was the last one viewed in that category (the settled choice) against a uniform expectation, how that compares with being viewed first (search-position bias), and the log number of such comparison sessions.
  r3p2_intraday_momentum_12h: Very short-term momentum just before the anchor. Column one compares distinct viewers in the last 12h with the same 12h of the previous day, normalised by global traffic. Column two is the item's share of cart/purchase actors in the last 12h against its 7d share, a share ratio so the global cart level cancels.
  r3p3_user_user_cf_acq: User-user collaborative filtering, unlike the existing item-item links: up to 30 neighbour users are found through overlap on the user's 10 latest items (niche items weighted by 1/log degree, hubs dropped), and their cart/purchase actions in the last 3 days are aggregated into a normalised weight share and a within-user rank.
  r3p4_brand_cat_price_sibling: Detects near-duplicate variants (colour or storage versions of the same model): for the top-50 products of the user's 3 latest category+brand combos, this gives the minimum log-price distance to items the user viewed in that same category and brand in the last 3 days, plus a price-and-recency kernel sum; this joint brand+category+price match is not captured by the separate ub_ and up_ features.
  r3p5_category_search_depth: How many distinct same-category items acquirers typically view before their first cart/purchase in the category (category-level log depth), and whether this item is a deliberated or an impulse choice relative to that norm; combined with uc_distinct_viewed_7d, it tells the model when a user's search is ripe to convert.
  r4p1_acq_regularity_7d: Steady sellers versus spiky ones. The first column counts the days in the last 7 on which the item had any cart/purchase actor. The second is the coefficient of variation of the item's daily share of all acquirers, so the cart logging level cancels.
  r4p2_cat_acq_concentration: Describes how winner-take-all the candidate's category is. It gives the Herfindahl index of the category's 7d cart/purchase actors over its items, plus the item's smoothed log share and log rank inside its category for all products in the category, not only those of the user's top categories.
  r4p3_weekday_seasonality_14d: Weekly seasonality for the target day: the item's and its category's share of cart/purchase actors on the same weekday 7 and 14 days ago, relative to their 14d share. It is normalised by global daily acquirers so the cart shift cancels.
  r4p4_cat_to_item_transition: Category-to-item transition: the smoothed lift of P(user acquires item p after first viewing category c) over the base rate, learned over 14 days. It is applied from the user's 3 latest viewed categories to the top 25 items per category, which generalises the item-level view->acquisition links to whole-category browsing, including cross-category pulls.
  r4p5_brand_switch_transition: Within-category brand transitions: the smoothed lift of P(acquire brand b2 in category c after viewing brand b1 there) over the category base rate, covering both loyalty and switching. It is applied from the user's 2 latest viewed category+brand pairs to the top 15 acquired items of the 3 best follow-up brands.
  r5p1_cf_xday_new_acq_transition: Item-item links that copy the target's structure: the smoothed lift of P(a user first touches and acquires item b within 24h on day d | the user viewed item a on day d-1 or d-2), learned over 14 days and applied from the user's 20 latest views of the last 48h; the target is new items acquired the next day, whereas the existing view->acquisition links are untimed.
  r5p2_cf_next_view_adjacency: Directional sequential links instead of whole-session co-occurrence: the probability that the next distinct item viewed in a session after a is b (7d, view-only, so the cart shift does not affect it), plus its lift over the popularity expectation, applied from the user's 5 latest viewed items with positional decay. This captures the click paths of the 'similar items' widget.
  r5p3_cf_converting_session_coview: Co-view cosine computed only over 14d sessions of 2-40 items that ended in a cart or purchase, so links reflect alternatives that were seriously weighed against each other in a buying decision rather than casual browsing. It is applied to the user's 10 latest views with a 48h recency decay.
  r5p4_uc_nextday_continuation: A category-specific and state-conditional conversion rate. It is learned as P(a user acquires in category c one or two days after viewing k distinct items of c on a day), with k bucketed by log2, and expressed as a lift over the global rate. It is evaluated at the user's own depth and lag in their 3 latest categories, then attached to the top 30 acquired products of each category. A tree cannot get this from uc_distinct_viewed and the category popularity features without knowing the category id.
  r5p5_cf_considered_seed_coview: Long-memory co-view seeds. It uses the user's 5 items with the most distinct revisit sessions over 14 days (at least 2), i.e. products they kept coming back to, which often fall outside the 20-latest-views window. It takes their 7d session co-view neighbours and weights them by log revisit count and a 96h recency decay. This extends the near-miss acquisition-seed idea to strong, uncommitted consideration.
  r6p1_older_view_seed_coview: Long-memory co-view: session co-view neighbours (7d, sessions of 2-30 items) of the user's distinct viewed items ranked 21-40 by recency over 14 days, which fall just outside the existing 20-latest-views window, weighted by a 120h recency decay; this extends the earlier revisit-seed idea to the rest of the user's older browsing.
  r6p2_dwell_seed_coview: Consideration seeds selected by attention rather than revisits or recency: the user's 5 items with the largest total page dwell (time to the next event in the session, capped at 300 s, at least 60 s total) over 7 days, expanded to their 7d session co-view neighbours and weighted by log dwell and a 72h decay.
  r6p3_abandoned_cart_seed_coview: Substitute search after an abandoned cart: session co-view neighbours (7d) of the user's 5 latest items that were carted in the last 7 days but never purchased, with a 48h recency decay. An unfinished cart signals an open need that the user may close with an alternative, unlike purchased seeds, which mix in complements.
  r6p4_user_level_cooccurrence_cf: Cross-session item-item links: a cosine over users (with 2-60 distinct viewed items in 14 days) who viewed both items at any time, not necessarily in the same session. It is applied to the user's 5 latest views of the last 48h with a 24h decay, and it captures items people return to compare on later days, which session co-view misses.
  r6p5_fresh_2d_coview_graph: A fresh co-view graph built only from the last 48h of sessions (2-30 items), applied to the user's 5 latest views with a 12h decay. It catches links that only formed in the last two days around new launches, promotions or restocks, which are diluted in the 7-14d graphs; it is view-only, so it is immune to the cart shift.
